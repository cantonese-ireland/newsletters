😢😄 Occasions for sorrow, occasions for joy 😄😢

Emotions are an important part of life. Happiness, sadness, anger, fear, excitement, nervousness, disappointment... the list goes on. It is invaluable for all of us to learn to recognise and embrace them as an inseparable part of our being.

Join us this Saturday for a cosy online bilingual storytelling session in Cantonese and English featuring Wilf the Wolf Laughs and Cries.

We're also delighted to welcome Heidi as our storyteller this weekend. With a background in early childhood education and a love for storytelling, Heidi will bring the story to life and encourage children to join in the fun.

Please prepare a pencil ✏️ (or a pen 🖊️) for your children!

📚 Bilingual storytelling in Cantonese & English
💻 Online
📅 This Saturday. 7th Nov. 11-1140am.

😢😄 哭有時，笑有時 😄😢

情緒係生活中重要嘅一部分。開心、悲傷、憤怒、恐懼、興奮、緊張、失望……。我哋所有人都應該學習認識同埋接受我哋嘅情緒為生活不可分割嘅一部分。

今個星期六，我哋誠邀大家參加粵英雙語網上故事時間，一齊欣賞《又喊又笑》（Wilf the Wolf Laughs and Cries）。

今次我哋好高興仲邀請到擁有豐富幼兒教育經驗嘅 Heidi老師 擔任故事姐姐！由佢對講故事嘅熱愛所帶動，Heidi老師會用生動有趣嘅方式帶領小朋友進入故事世界，一齊互動、分享同享受閱讀樂趣。

請各位家長為小朋友準備一支鉛筆 ✏️（或者原子筆 🖊️）！
