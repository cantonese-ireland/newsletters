🎃 Did You Know Halloween Has Irish Roots?

Halloween traces its origins back over 2,000 years to the ancient Celtic festival of Samhain in Ireland, marking the end of summer and the beginning of winter. People lit bonfires and wore costumes to ward off spirits. Irish emigrants later brought these traditions to America, helping shape the Halloween celebrations we know today!

🎃 你知唔知道萬聖節起源愛爾蘭？

萬聖節嘅歷史可以追溯到二千幾年前愛爾蘭古代凱爾特人嘅重要節慶 Samhain (愛爾蘭文︰十一月)，象徵夏天結束、冬天開始。當時凱爾特人會燃點篝火、戴上面具驅趕鬼魂。後來愛爾蘭移民將呢啲傳統帶到美國，慢慢演變成今日大家熟悉嘅萬聖節！👻🍀

🌍 Building Bridges Through Advocacy: CII at Campaign for Uyghurs Workshop

In mid-September, our Chairperson Max Yu delivered a workshop and talk at the Campaign for Uyghurs Advocacy Training Workshop.

His session, “Marketing in a Politically Polarised World,” explored how social media can help mobilise supporters, drawing on research and practical examples to share effective communication strategies.

At Cantonese in Ireland, we believe in supporting not only our own community but also sharing knowledge and building connections with other communities.

A sincere thank you to Irish Uyghur Cultural Association (IUCA), Nuria Zyden and Campaign for Uyghurs for the warm welcome and opportunity to contribute!

🌍 跨社群交流，攜手倡議：粵語愛爾蘭參與維吾爾族倡議工作坊

9月中旬，粵語愛爾蘭主席 Max Yu 獲邀參與 Campaign for Uyghurs 倡議培訓工作坊，主持工作坊並作專題分享。

Max 以 「政治兩極化世界下的市場營銷」 為題，結合研究同實際案例，分享點樣善用社交媒體凝聚支持者，以及喺政治爭議環境下有效溝通嘅實用技巧。

粵語愛爾蘭除咗為本地粵語社群發聲，亦希望透過分享知識同經驗，促進唔同社群之間嘅交流、合作同互助。

衷心感謝 Irish Uyghur Cultural Association (IUCA)、Nuria Zyden 同 Campaign for Uyghurs 嘅熱情接待同邀請！
