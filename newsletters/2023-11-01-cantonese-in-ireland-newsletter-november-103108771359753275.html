Cantonese Conversation and Writing Course 廣東話會話及寫作班

Discover the comfort of learning Cantonese from the cozy confines of your home! Our online Cantonese course focuses primarily on conversational skills and writing—the two essential elements of language mastery. With the course fully online, all you need is a comfortable sofa, a cup of brimming coffee, and an adventurous spirit to embark on refreshing 1-hour sessions.

Taught by an experienced Cantonese language instructor and backed by Cantonese In Ireland, this course promises an authentic learning experience.

COURSE DETAILS
Dates:
November:14, 21, 28
December: 5, 12,19
January: 16, 23, 30
Time: Every Tuesday evening (8:30pm-9:30pm)
Class Size: Enjoy small, personalised classes with no more than 15 students.
Tuition fee: €65 per person
Teaching language: English
Platform: Zoom

The course will be theme based and conversation based. There will be time for conversation between classmates. Character recognition, writing and Cantonese slangs will be taught.

About our language instructor:
Andy Fung is a qualified secondary school teacher from Hong Kong. He has 16 years of teaching experience, including teaching Cantonese as a Second Language to non-native learners. Newly migrated to Ireland, Andy enjoys travelling, painting, gardening and cycling in his spare time.

Photo Exhibition: Flowerboards in Ireland

Flowerboard Photo Exhibition displays photographs of traditional flowerboards taken by our Cantonese Irish. It also displays the photographs taken by professional photographer Louis Tang during our Flowerboard Workshops held in Cork City Library at the Culture Night Weekend. 

The flowerboard crafting technique is one of the intangible cultural heritage in Hong Kong. Our Irish public will learn through the exhibition the history and the craftsmanship of this traditional Cantonese art form. We invite the audiences to come experience and be inspired by this exotic art form of the East.

Time： starting from 11 November 2023 till the end of November
Venue: Cork City Library


相片展覽︰花牌製作在愛爾蘭

喺九月二十二號及二十三號，粵語愛爾蘭喺高基市圖書館舉辦咗花牌製作工作坊。而喺十一月呢，我哋就會有「花牌相片展覽」。

相片展覽會展示專業攝影師 Louis Tang 喺花牌工作坊期間影嘅相。仲會介紹花牌嘅由來、組件、歷史等。

「花牌紮作技藝」係香港其中一個非物質文化遺產。喺香港，紅白二事例如節慶、婚禮、開幕、喪禮等都會見到唔同色彩嘅花牌。想知更多嘅朋友，記住十一月嚟高基市圖書館睇吓喇。

時間：2023年11月4日至11月底
地點：高基市圖書館
