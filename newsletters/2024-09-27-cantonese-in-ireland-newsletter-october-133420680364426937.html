Flowerboards Photo Exhibition


Date: 30th Sep to 5th Oct 2024

Venue: Charleville Mall Library, Dublin 1


The tradtional craftsmanship of crafting flowerboards is one of Hong Kong's most treasured intangible cultural heritage. They are large, colourful and festive boards used to decorate celebrations of the birthday of deities, the opening of new buildings and shops, and other big events.

This public exhibition displays photographs of traditional flowerboards and a number of flowerboards created in multiple flowerboard making workshops organised by us in Culture Night 2023 and Lunar New Year celebration 2024, as well as photographs from the workshops taken by Louis Tang.

We invite you to come experience the history and craftsmanship of flowerboards. See you there!

Flowerboards Photo Exhibition is one of the events in Dublin Festival of History


花牌藝術計畫攝影展覽


日期：2024年9月30號-10月5號

場地：Charleville Mall Library, Dublin 1


花牌紮作呢個傳統手工藝喺香港其中一樣最受珍藏嘅非物質遺產。呢啲大型花牌，色彩繽紛，充滿節日氣氛。花牌通常用作慶祝神誕節慶、開張誌慶、以及其他大型場合嘅裝飾。

Free Online Lecture: The History of the Cantonese Diaspora in Ireland

Join us for a special online lecture exploring the history of Cantonese migration to Ireland. Long before the Celtic Tiger era, the first Cantonese migrants, largely from Hong Kong, arrived in Ireland in the late 1950s and early 1960s. Over the decades, this community has evolved—from business owners in the catering industry to international students and critical skill professionals, including second-generation Irish Cantonese.

Whether you're part of the diaspora, interested in multicultural studies, or curious about the impact of the Cantonese community on Irish society, this lecture will be both educational and entertaining.


Date: 9th Oct 2024

Time: 8-9pm

Format: Online (conducted in English)

Speaker: Alice Chau, nearfm90.3 presenter, business owner and Cantonese immigrant

Don’t miss this unique opportunity to learn about the experiences, challenges, and contributions of the Cantonese-speaking community in Ireland!

This online lecture is one of the events in Dublin Festival of History


免費網上講座：愛爾蘭粵移民歷史

透過呢個網上講座，你可以了解愛爾蘭粵移民嘅歷史。早喺「凱爾特之虎」經濟起飛之前，第一批粵移民喺1950年代尾至1960年代初已經移居愛爾蘭，呢批粵移民大部分嚟自香港。經過幾十年，呢個社群慢慢演變——由主要經營餐飲業嘅商人，變成國際學生同埋專業技術人士，仲有第二代粵移民，以愛爾蘭為家，身份認同為愛爾蘭人。

無論你係呢個社群嘅一份子，抑或對多元文化研究有興趣，定係對粵移民社群對愛爾蘭嘅影響感到好奇，呢個好有趣味講座都會令你獲益良多。


日期：2024年10月9號

時間︰夜晚 8-9點

形式：網上講座（以英語進行）

講者：Alice Chau，Asian Talents at 90.3 Near FM 節目主持人、商界人士，本身亦係一位粵移民

大家唔好錯過呢個獨特嘅機會，了解粵社群喺愛爾蘭嘅經歷、挑戰同埋貢獻！

係都柏林歷史節嘅其中一個活動。

National Integration Conference

Cantonese in Ireland was invited to attend the National Integration Conference on 25 September 2024 which took place at Gresham Hotel. The conference lasted from 8:30am to 5:30pm, and it was filled with insightful discussions, workshops and networking opportunities focused on migrant integration.

This year’s topic is Leaving No One Behind - Working towards effective Migrant Inclusion. The aim is to bring together policy-makers, community leaders, and social advocates to explore innovative strategies to ensure that no migrant is excluded.

As always, Cantonese in Ireland represented a linguistic and cultural minority at this event. Our representatives played a part in the great diversity of the event.


國家隔合會議

粵語愛爾蘭於9月25號2024年被邀請出席由愛爾蘭政府舉辦嘅國家融合會議。呢個大會喺 Gresham Hotel 舉行，朝早8點半開始，黃昏5點半完結。大會期間，大家一齊進行有意義嘅討論，仲有有用嘅工作坊，係一同愛爾蘭各團體建立關係嘅機會。會議焦點放喺移民融入社會方面。

今年嘅主題係「Leaving No One Behind - Working towards effective Migrant Inclusion」。目的係聯同政治決策者、社會領袖同埋各方支持者一齊探索創新策略，確保所有移民都可以融入社會。

粵語愛爾蘭喺呢個大會代表一個語言同埋文化小眾。我哋嘅代表亦都出一分力，為大會增添從粵人士考量嘅角度。
