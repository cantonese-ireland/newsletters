Cantonese course for non-native speakers 

粵語初班（非母語人士）

FREE online Cantonese course for non-native speakers aged 15 and above. The class is sponsored by Dublin Bus Community Spirit Award.

Andy Fung is a qualified secondary school teacher from Hong Kong. He is also very experienced in teaching Cantonese as a second language to non-native learners.

免費網上粵語課程，專為非以粵語為母語、十五歲或以上嘅人士而設。課程由都柏林巴士社區精神獎項贊助。

我哋嘅導師馮老師係一位嚟自香港嘅註冊中學老師，有非常豐富教授非以粵語為母語學生嘅經驗。

Funded by DCEDIY, this is a follow-up event after Migrant Political Participation Workshop. We will gather together to register to vote, have a cuppa and chat. Probably some time in mid-May, stay tuned for more details later. 

由 DCEDIY 贊助嘅《由投開始愛新活》 係上一次嘅 《來愛新活》 嘅延續篇，我哋將會藉住呢個機會同大家傾偈飲茶同埋登記做選民，時間暫定為五月中，詳情請繼續留意我哋嘅消息。

I love you我愛你ngo5 oi3 nei5

Thank you to Cork City Council for having us at the Cork City Centre St. Patrick’s Day Parade. We had so much fun with the public saying I love you in Cantonese to each other. Special thanks to Irene Yip Millinery for the handmade traditional flower boards. 

See you next year! 

多謝高基市議會俾我哋今日同大家一齊參加喺高基市中心嘅St. Patrick’s Day遊行。我哋同大眾「我愛你」「你愛我」咁愛嚟愛去，真有愛。

特別鳴謝Irene Yip Millinery 親手整嘅傳統花牌。下年見！

Workshop on Migrant Political Participation had a great turnout and participation. We are looking forward to seeing everyone at the follow-up event to register to vote and chat! 

來愛新活：認識投票權利與制度已順利完成。我哋希望咁多位參與者可以繼續參與呢個活動嘅延續篇 《由投開始愛新活》。

Guilty Conscience has been a great success! It was wonderful to be surrounded by Cantonese speakers. The excitement was phenomenal!  We are looking forward to seeing more Cantonese language movies on the big screen in Ireland.

喺愛爾蘭首度公演嘅 《毒舌大狀》取得非常美滿嘅成績！好耐都未試過咁多講粵語嘅朋友坐埋一齊，電影播放嘅時候大家都非常興奮。我哋希望會有更多嘅粵語電影可以喺愛爾蘭播放。

Delighted to see a Cantonese movie - Blue Island, was included in the line up for the East Asia Film Festival Ireland. A lovely surprise to see 2nd gen Hong Kong Irish film-maker Diana Cheung and 2nd gen Hong Kong Irish actor Vincent Tsang join our meet-up. 

入圍愛爾蘭東亞電影節嘅憂鬱之島喺愛爾蘭都有得睇啦，非常榮幸請到本身係愛爾蘭第二代香港人嘅導演 Diana Cheung 同埋演員 Vincent Tsang 參與我哋嘅聚會。

Cork City Library Bilingual Storytelling 高基市圖書館雙語故事時間

Bilingual Storytelling in Cork City Library. We will listen to Cantonese songs, do some arts and crafts or learn to write Traditional Chinese characters.

From now to Jun 2023, every 2nd Sat at 11 am. No need to register. Just show up. 

我哋將會喺高基市圖書館以雙語同大家講故事，仲會同大家一齊聽吓歌仔，做吓手工，寫吓繁體字。

由宜家至 2023 年 6 月，每月第二個星期六上午 11 點。無需報名，自行出現即可。

    As you can see from this newsletter, we have worked very hard to promote Cantonese culture in Ireland and support Cantonese migrants to integrate into Irish society. Since our start in 2022, all the work has been done by volunteers. Even though we have a small amount of public funds, our regular expenses rely on your support. If you want to see our work continue to contribute to Ireland, please help us so that we can continue this important work. 

    大家都睇到，我哋不斷努力致力於愛爾蘭推動粵語語言及文化，亦希望幫助粵語移民融入愛爾蘭生活。本會成立以嚟，單靠義工及各善長人翁出錢出力，雖然我哋開始有政府及其他機構嘅贊助，但我哋嘅年度開支，都仍然須靠大眾嘅捐款支持。如果你想見到我哋繼續係本地付出，請各位多多支持，等大家可以繼續一齊走落去。
