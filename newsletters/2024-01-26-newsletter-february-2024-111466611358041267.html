Our Volunteers 我哋嘅義工

In this issue, we would like to introduce you our volunteer - Angel. 

喺今期嘅通訊，我哋想同大家介紹吓我哋嘅義工Angel。

Tell us something about yourself.  What brought you here?

By chance, I came to Ireland to work. So far, I have lived in Ireland for 32 months. I have tried different things I wouldn't have the chance to do in Hong Kong, such as sketching.

Why do you volunteer with Cantonese in ireland ? What do you do in the organisation?

When I came to Ireland, I found that compared to other European countries, Ireland has a smaller population of Hong Kong immigrants. In Ireland, the support for Hong Kong immigrants is little. I discovered that Cantonese in Ireland is an organisation which is dedicated to bridge the Cantonese immigrants and local Irish people, so I volunteered myself to help. I hope I can use my past experience and, when time permits (outside of working hours), to contribute to help other Hong Kong people in Ireland

What are your hopes for the development of Cantonese language and culture in Ireland?

English is the common language here. If Cantonese can be accepted in Ireland like other languages ​​(e.g. Spanish, French), it will help Hong Kong people in Ireland, especially the elderly, to build a sense of belonging to Ireland.

不如你介紹吓自己。乜解究你嚟咗愛爾蘭？

機緣巧合之下，我黎到愛爾蘭工作。到目前為止，已經喺愛爾蘭生活咗32個月喇。喺呢度，我嘗試咗喺香港唔會做既野，例如︰寫生。

點解你會喺我哋<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>呢個機構做義工嘅？你喺機構度負責啲乜嘢？

我嚟到愛爾蘭，發覺對比起其他歐洲國家，愛爾蘭嘅香港人口比例都係較少。喺愛爾蘭，暫時提供俾初黎報到嘅香港人嘅支援仍有待發展。咁啱發現呢個會都係做緊呢類型嘅野，所以我加入咗呢個機構，希望自己可以以過來人嘅經歷，喺時間許可下 (工作時間以外)，出啲力，幫到其他喺愛爾蘭嘅香港人。

你對愛爾蘭嘅粵語和粵語文化發展有何期望？

英文係呢度嘅流通語言。如果廣東話能夠好似其他語言 (例如︰西班牙語、法語) 一樣喺愛爾蘭共存，咁會幫助喺愛爾蘭嘅香港人，特別係長者，建立對愛爾蘭嘅歸屬感。
