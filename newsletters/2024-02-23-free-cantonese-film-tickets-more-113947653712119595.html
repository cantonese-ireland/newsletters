Free Film Tickets for you 免費戲飛送俾你

Another Hong Kong film ‘Elegies’, directed by one of the renowned Hong Kong filmmakers (Ann Hui), is selected for the 8th East Asia Film Festival Ireland (5-10 March2024). It will be shown in Irish Film Institute (IFI) on 10th March 5.45pm.

THANKS to East Asia Film Festival Ireland, two free tickets are given to Cantonese in Ireland.

‘Elegies’, is an ode to the city of Hong Kong in a state of transition, devoted to poets of the ‘old’ generation (born 1937–1952), through to the poets of today. Hui reveals the topography of contemporary poetry, while giving us glimpses of inner life in Hong Kong through the movie 'Elegies'. (notes by Marie-Pierre Richard, EAFFI programmer & artistic director)

Céilí night in Richmond Barracks, Inchicore 愛爾蘭舞之夜

A night of traditional Irish dances guided by céilí caller and sean nós dancer Louise O’Connor. 

What to Expect: Louise will be your dance maestro, leading you through every step. 

No dance experience needed – perfect for beginners. 

Live music to keep your feet tapping and spirits high.

Céilí night is a great opportunity to try something new and connect with others through the joy of dance. 

Bring your comfortable shoes. 

Be prepared to have a lot of fun. 

Venue: Richmond Barracks 

Date: Wednesday, 6 March 

Time: 7 pm 

Admission: FREE 

Note: This is an alcohol-free event for adults/over 18s.
