Dragon Boat Race Watching in Carlow 2024

Hey, it's time for the annual Dragon Boat Festival again.

This year is the second year that our team Cheung Po Tsai has participated in the dragon boat competition organised by Irish Dragon Boat Association.

We sincerely invite you to visit Carlow to watch the dragon boat competition and cheer us up.

Venue: River Barrow, Carlow Town Park, Graiguecullen, Co. Carlow

Fee: FREE

Date: 9 Jun 2024 (Sunday)

Time: Eye Dotting Ceremony at 9:30, first race starts at 11:00. Our last race at 15:00 but the whole race finishes at around 18:00 if you wish to watch other teams. 

How do get there? You can drive to Carlow, or there is public transport (bus / train ) from Dublin to Carlow.

端午節 Carlow 龍舟觀賽又到咗一年一度既端午節啦。

今年係我哋 << 張保仔龍舟隊>> 第二年參加由 Irish Dragon Boat Association 舉辦嘅龍舟比賽。我哋誠意邀請你過嚟Carlow觀賞龍舟賽，同我哋打氣支持我哋。

地點︰Carlow鎮公園既River Barrow

費用︰免費

日期︰六月九號 (星期日)

時間︰ 點睛儀式上晝9:30，第一場賽事上晝十一點。 張保仔最後一場賽事下午3:00 ，但如果你想睇說其他隊伍嘅比賽要大約去到下午6:00

點去嗰度？你可以開車啦，又或者坐巴士、火車都得㗎。

Cantonese movie in CORK!

Great news! We finally get to watch the recently released Hong Kong movie in Cork!

Twilight of the Warriors: Walled In has finally reached Cork City!

2 cinemas: Arc Cinema, Cork and Cork Omniplex

From 14th to 20th June. 

Kowloon Walled City is world-famous. Although the buildings of the Walled City have disappeared, the legend of the Walled City continues to be circulated. This movie is very interesting, and the scene reconstructions are amazing. Of course, there are also Hong Kong martial arts that you can’t miss.

Arc Cinema, Cork: https://cork.arccinema.ie/event/89042 (You can make the booking now!)

Cork Omniplex: https://www.omniplex.ie/cinema/cork (pre-order starts on June 7th).

Local Election 7 June! 六月七日地區選舉！

Hey everyone! Have you checked your mailbox lately? If you're registered to vote and eligible for the upcoming elections on June 7th, you should start receiving your polling information card!

Don't forget to check on checktheregister.ie to see whether you are really registered, you might also want to know about the candidates in your area.

Remember, you can only vote at the polling station listed on your card, so keep it handy and follow the instructions provided!

Let's make our voices heard—don't miss out on this important opportunity to vote! 

大家收到投票通知卡未呀？大家最近有冇檢查你的郵箱？如果你已經登記做選民，並符合6月7日即將舉行的選舉資格，你應該開始收到投票通知卡了！

請記住去checktheregister.ie睇吓有無登記到。另外，如果覺得好頭痕，唔知自己選區有邊個候選人。有個網站可以幫到你。

請記住，你只能夠喺卡上指定投票站投票，所以請妥善保管並按照卡上的指示進行！讓我地嘅聲音被聽到——切勿錯過呢個重要嘅投票機會！

Report 工作報告

New friends and old friends came together in our anniversary celebration, speaking in Cantonese, feeling so good.

Cantonese in Ireland is a not for profit organization. All work is done by a group of volunteers. In the past two years, we have done a lot of activities, many people from the Irish public who want to contact the Cantonese community would approach us to seek help.

We have a small award ceremony as well, and we would like to express our thanks to our four volunteers who have worked very hard from the beginning when we set up the organisation:

Heidi - Works with Rhyse to publish weekly cultural posts non-stop. We lost count the number of posts Cantonese in Ireland has published in the last two years. Heidi is also one of the best tutors of our storytelling team as well.

Rhyse - A creative leader of the Cultural Promotion Group. Once he receives the request to do a post to promote an event, he runs in automatic mode that we know things would get done beautifully.

Christine - You can see Christine every month in the Cork City Library Storytelling. She's there to greet, to assist, to support. For all the events in Cork, she's there. She even plans her holiday around the events and brings her husband along to help and support. 

Janus - One of the founders of the organisation, Janus helps with countless things and even asked her daughter to help.Many thanks to the above four and all the other volunteers.We would also like to thank Hong Kong Taste and Ka Shing Chinese Restaurant for generously sponsoring our yummy food and drinks!

週年慶典完滿結束，新知舊雨一齊大講特講廣東話，真係令人心情暢快。

<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>係一個非牟利團體，所有工作都係由一班義工落手落腳做，我哋無任何大台支持，做咗兩年已經做咗好多活動出嚟，有唔少外界人士如果想要聯絡廣東話羣體，都會搵我哋。

琴日我哋有個小小頒獎典禮，特別多謝四位由成立到而家都盡心盡力嘅義工︰

Heidi - 同Rhyse一齊無間斷出每週嘅文化post，兩年幾都唔知出咗幾多個post，亦係我哋故事組皇牌導師之一。

Rhyse - 文化宣傳部隊長，文字創意擔當，全自動波唔駛叫就做嘢，交親啲任務俾佢都唔駛追佢做。

Christine - 每個月嘅圖書館講故事都喺度，所有Cork 活動都會見到Christine 落力招呼，執頭執尾，仲帶埋老公一齊做，放假都會就埋組織活動。

Janus - 創會元老之一，佢幫手做嘅嘢真係多到數唔到，仲會叫埋個女一齊幫手做。

多謝以上四位，以及其他義工。我哋仲要多謝 Hong Kong Taste 同埋 Ka Shing Chinese Restaurant 慷慨贊助我哋美食飲品！
