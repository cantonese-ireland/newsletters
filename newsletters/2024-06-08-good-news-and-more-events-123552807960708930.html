Recently released Cantonese movie reaches Galway

'Twilight of the Warriors: Walled In' has finally reached Cork City and Salthill Galway. 

The first time ever that we can watch a newly released Cantonese movie in these two cities!

And more screenings in Dublin as well.

Kowloon Walled City is world-famous. Although the buildings of the Walled City have disappeared, the legend of the Walled City continues to be circulated. 

This movie is very entertaining, and the scene reconstructions are amazing. 

Of course, there are also Hong Kong martial arts, Hong Kong flowerboards and many more HK elements that you can’t miss.

There is English and Mandarin Chinese subtitles. Call your friends to watch it together! 


新上粵語電影去埋高威市

<<九龍城寨之圍城>>終於打到去高基市同埋高威市喇！

第一次有新上粵語片去到呢兩個城市。

都柏林戲院亦都加咗場次。

九龍城寨聞名於世，雖然城寨建築已經消失，但城寨傳說卻繼續流傳。呢部電影非常有趣，場景重建都似得驚人。當然，仲有你不能錯過嘅香港武打、香港花牌同埋其他香港元素。

套戲有英文及漢字華語字幕，叫埋朋友一齊睇啦！
