Bloomsday Photo Competition 2025
2025 布盧姆日攝影比賽


Celebrate James Joyce’s Dublin through your camera lens! Whether you’re joining our Cantonese walking tour or visiting the sites on your own, you can enter our Bloomsday Photo Competition!

同James Joyce 一樣，用你嘅作品説話！無論你係參加我哋嘅導賞團，定係自己得閒嘅時候去影，只要你影相嘅地點係喺我哋指定路線上都可以參加今次比賽！


Take photos at one or more of our chosen spots, including the National Library of Ireland, Trinity College Dublin and O'Connell Bridge. You can access the full list here.

指定攝影地點包括愛爾蘭國家圖書館、聖三一學院、及奧康諾橋。完整路線圖在此。


Open to all residents of the ROI, and all ages are welcome. The top 3 entries will receive €50, €30 and €20 respectively. Additionally, the top 10 entries will be publicly displayed on the Nassau Street Wall at Trinity College Dublin for two weeks! 

比賽歡迎所有愛爾蘭嘅居民參加，年齡不限。最佳3名會分別獲得€50、€30及€20獎金。最佳10份作品將會聖三一學院 Nassau Street 外牆展出兩個禮拜！


Our judge is Louis Tang, a cinematographer at Noirxtone limited. Louis is a German-born Cantonese multimedia artist whose journey spans Germany, the UK, and Ireland. Deeply inspired by 80s–90s Hong Kong cinema and film photography, he has since built a name as one of Ireland’s few East Asian commercial photographers.

我哋嘅評審喺 Louis Tang，Noirxtone 嘅攝影導演。Louis 係一位喺德國出世嘅粵語多媒體創作人，佢喺德國、英國同愛爾蘭都有屬於佢嘅創作。Louis深受 80-90 年代嘅香港電影同埋電影攝影啟發，令佢喺愛爾蘭成功建立自己嘅名聲，成為呢度少有嘅東亞商業攝影師之一。


Competition Rules:

  • No AI-generated images
  • High-quality photos only (no blurry shots!)
  • Entries will be judged by Louis Tang. All decisions are final. We reserve the right to disqualify any entry that is deemed inappropriate or does not conform to stated contest rules.
  • By entering, you agree your photo may be used for promotion

比賽規則：

  • 唔接受 AI 圖像
  • 相片要夠清、夠靚！
  • 參賽作品由 Louis Tang 評審。所有結果為最終決定，恕不接受上訴。我哋有權取消任何被視為不適當或唔符合比賽規則嘅參賽作品資格
  • 相片可供粵語愛爾蘭會用作推廣用途


The deadline for submission is on 31 July 2025.

截止日期喺2025年7月31號。


This event is kindly sponsored by Dublin City Council.

今次活動由Dublin City Council贊助

One Good Day Open Submission Call 美好一天藝術創作徵集

Another opportunities for your creativity to shine.

Have you ever had one good day you still carry in your heart? A moment of joy, peace, or hope that deserves to be seen and heard? Now’s your chance. Submissions are OPEN for ONE GOOD DAY, a new arts journal by Good Day, Cork — and they want your voice in it.Creators from the Cantonese community in Ireland and beyond are warmly encouraged to submit — the journal aims to amplify voices from underrepresented groups.Whether you're a writer, poet, or visual artist — share your story, your art, your perspective.

Submit prose, poetry, or black-and-white visual art.

Deadline: 1st September

Prose in English, Poetry can be in Cantonese (with English translation)

No fees, no AI, and anonymous selection. 

All featured artists get a free copy of the journal, another zine, and social media promo.

Let your good day inspire someone else. 

Follow Good Day, Cork & @eganreeve (on instagram) or email info.gooddaycork@gmail.com with questions.

Open Doors' Future Leader programme 未來領袖訓練

Just over a week left to apply for The Open Doors Initiative's Future Leader programme. A 12-month mentorship programme designed to help you achieve your career aspirations, develop new skills, and reach your full potential. Apply by Friday 1st August 2025.

As part of Future Leaders, you’ll have the opportunity to:

- Advance your career path with personalised guidance from an experienced mentor.

- Build or refine essential skills that align with your goals.

- Set and achieve meaningful milestones, with a clear plan and ongoing support.

- Overcome challenges and unlock your potential with actionable strategies.

This programme is not aimed at job seekers or those starting their careers. This programme is tailored to support professionals who have been working in Ireland for at least one year, who are looking for support in developing their careers, acquiring new skills, or achieving specific goals. Please complete this form to apply for the programme

「未來領袖計劃（Future Leader Programme）」剩返一個星期就截止申請喇！呢個係為期12個月嘅導師指導計劃，旨在協助你實現職業目標、發展新技能，並發揮你嘅潛力。申請截止日期為2025年8月1日（星期五）。

參加「未來領袖計劃」，你將有機會：

  • 由經驗豐富嘅導師提供個人指導，幫助你建構你嘅職業發展道路。

  • 建立或改善同你目標相關嘅重要技能，

  • 設定並達成有意義嘅里程碑，擁有清晰嘅計劃和持續支援。

  • 面對挑戰時獲得具體策略指導，幫助發揮你嘅潛能。

此計劃並非針對正喺度搵工作或剛開始職業生涯嘅人士。此計劃專為喺愛爾蘭工作至少一年、有意進一步發展職涯、學習新技能或達成具體目標嘅專業人士而設。
