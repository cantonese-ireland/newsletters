Due to popular demand, more Cantonese lessons! 
詢眾要求，加開粵語班！

FREE online Cantonese course for non-native speakers aged 15 and above. The class is sponsored by Dublin Bus Community Spirit Award.

免費網上粵語課程，專為非以粵語為母語、十五歲或以上嘅人士而設。課程由都柏林巴士社區精神獎項贊助。

Andy Fung is a qualified secondary school teacher from Hong Kong. He is also very experienced teaching Cantonese as a second language to non-native learners.

我哋嘅導師馮老師係一位嚟自香港嘅註冊中學老師，有非常豐富教授非以粵語為母語學生嘅經驗。

In this course, Andy will introduce you to the commonly used Cantonese sentence structures and phrases. He will teach you the tips to analyse the Traditional Chinese characters through the etymology of Hanzi. Using jyutping, the romanisation system of Cantonese language, you will be able to read the Cantonese words and sentences easily. Andy will also require you to practise and speak with the other learners in the class.

喺呢個課程入面，馮老師會教你一啲常見嘅粵語句式及字詞。佢又會教你漢字演變，以拆解漢字結構，要記住繁體漢字就話咁易。透過學習粵拼，你亦都會好快掌握粵語發音。馮老師會要求每位同學課堂期間都要開口作口語練習。

About our tutor 關於我哋嘅導師:

Andy Fung is a qualified secondary school teacher from Hong Kong. He has 16 years of teaching experience, including teaching Cantonese as a Second Language to non-native learners. Newly migrated to Ireland, Andy enjoys travelling, painting, gardening and cycling in his spare time.

馮老師係一位嚟自香港嘅註冊中學老師。佢擁有十六年教學經驗，包括向非以粵語為母語嘅學生教授粵語。馮老師啱啱移民到愛爾蘭無耐，喺餘閒時間，非常鍾意旅遊、油畫、園藝及踩單車。
