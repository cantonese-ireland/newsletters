Do you know you can still vote even you are not yet a citizen in Ireland? Do you know much about our Irish political system, citizens’ roles, and election system? 

Cantonese in Ireland is thrilled to collaborate with Immigrant Council of Ireland, co-hosting a virtual workshop on ‘Migrant Political Participation’. The professionals will explain in simple languages and help you to be involved in Ireland in different political ways!

It is held online in a bilingual setting — English and Cantonese. And it’s FREE.

你知唔知就算你未成為愛爾蘭公民都有選票？你對愛爾蘭認識有幾多？你認識愛爾蘭嘅政治系統、我哋嘅市民權利、同埋愛爾蘭超複雜嘅選舉制度嗎？

<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>好開心可以同愛爾蘭移民委員會合作，共同舉辦網上工作坊。我哋專業嘅講者會用最簡單嘅語言教你最頭暈嘅概念，並教你點樣一步步做出行動，等你唔使拗晒頭。

工作坊以雙語（英語及粵語）進行，費用全免。

Mission Accomplished 工作報告

Great success of "Dinner in Irish Home" 

Dinner in Irish Home has been a great success. We have a total of 12 participants coming from 4 families. They get to experience Irish culture, food and friendship. Let's hear some of their testimonials...

Max:

‘Getting to learn more about Irish culture through cooking is a unique experience. It is always an honour to be invited to someone's home. The experience is a good mixture of cooking and eating. Ann taught us some classic Irish dishes. My favourite is Irish coffee. I picked up a new word, "craic". Thanks to the organisers and sponsors for this event.’

Vincent:

"This is really a wonderful night that my boys can cook together with Ann for an Irish dinner. We together with another family enjoyed so much. Besides, I also learnt the secret of how to make my own Irish coffee, which is really special and tasty. Hope this activity can be continued, as participants can learn more about Irish culture!"

Joanne:

"Thanks for arranging the Irish dinner. It is an amazing experience. Ann is really nice to teach us how to cook Irish food and the food is delicious too. My girl and I enjoyed the dinner very much."

<<愛爾蘭晚餐體驗>> 成功舉行

愛爾蘭晚餐體驗成功舉辦，總計有四個家庭及12名參加者參與呢次活動，佢哋今次不單對於愛爾蘭文化及食物加深左認識，而且仲認識咗愛爾蘭嘅朋友，我哋睇睇佢哋嘅意見：

Max:
今次係一個特別嘅經驗，尤其是從食物之中了解到愛爾蘭嘅文化。非常榮幸今次可以參加，今次嘅體驗除咗結合左煮同埋食之外，Ann亦都教咗我哋唔少愛爾蘭嘅菜式。我最鍾意就係愛爾蘭咖啡，我亦都學咗一個愛爾蘭嘅字”craic”。非常多謝主辦當局同埋贊助商。

Vincent:
今晚實在好奇妙，我嘅小朋友可以同Ann一齊煮愛爾蘭晚餐。我哋亦都同另一個參與家庭非常享受今晚，另外我都學識咗從愛爾蘭咖啡嘅秘技，希望呢個活動可以繼續落去，令到更多參加者學懂愛爾蘭文化

Joanne:
多謝安排今次嘅愛爾蘭晚餐，今晚嘅活動實在非常好。Ann好用心咁教我哋點樣烹調美味嘅愛爾蘭食物，我同我個女都非常享受今晚。

As you can see from this newsletter, we have worked very hard to promote Cantonese culture in Ireland and support Cantonese migrants to integrate into Irish society. Since our start in 2022, all the work has been done by volunteers. Even though we have a small amount of public funds, our regular expenses rely on your support. If you want to see our work continue to contribute to Ireland, please help us so that we can continue this important work. 

大家都睇到，我哋不斷努力致力於愛爾蘭推動粵語語言及文化，亦希望幫助粵語移民融入愛爾蘭生活。本會成立以嚟，單靠義工及各善長人翁出錢出力，雖然我哋開始有政府及其他機構嘅贊助，但我哋嘅年度開支，都仍然須靠大眾嘅捐款支持。如果你想見到我哋繼續係本地付出，請各位多多支持，等大家可以繼續一齊走落去。
