Work Experience Opportunity
25/06/2023

Dear Cantonese lovers, 

各位親愛嘅粵語愛好者︰


We are happy to announce that we have secured funding from North East Inner City - Dublin to implement a programme to help Cantonese speaking migrants to start their lives in Ireland. And we need two project managers to oversee the programme.


我哋好高興宣佈我哋成功爭取到 NEIC 贊助，將會推動一個幫助粵語移民適應愛爾蘭生活嘅計劃。我哋需要兩位項目經理幫手跟進計劃。

It will be a great opportunity for successful candidates to gain work experience with us. Related expenses will be covered and training will be provided to the right candidates.

成功嘅朋友，將會得到喺愛爾蘭工作嘅經驗。本機構會提供培訓，亦會補貼工作所需開支。
Details 詳情

As you can see from this newsletter, we have worked very hard to promote Cantonese culture in Ireland and support Cantonese migrants to integrate into Irish society. Since our start in 2022, all the work has been done by volunteers. Even though we have a small amount of public funds, our regular expenses rely on your support. If you want to see our work continue to contribute to Ireland, please help us so that we can continue this important work. 

大家都睇到，我哋不斷努力致力於愛爾蘭推動粵語語言及文化，亦希望幫助粵語移民融入愛爾蘭生活。本會成立以嚟，單靠義工及各善長人翁出錢出力，雖然我哋開始有政府及其他機構嘅贊助，但我哋嘅年度開支，都仍然須靠大眾嘅捐款支持。如果你想見到我哋繼續係本地付出，請各位多多支持，等大家可以繼續一齊走落去。
