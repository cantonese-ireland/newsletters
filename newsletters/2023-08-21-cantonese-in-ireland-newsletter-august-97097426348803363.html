Survey: Immigration Information Session (in Cantonese)
問卷調查︰移民講座 (粵語)

Cantonese in Ireland is planning to hold an information session for Cantonese immigrants, sponsored by Department of Children, Equality, Disability, Integration and Youth, to help them settle in Ireland and integrate in our society. The information session will probably happen at the end of September (30th September). We need your help to let us know what kind of information you need most in order to live in Ireland.

<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>正喺度籌備一個為粵語移民而設嘅移民講座，希望為你哋提供有用嘅資訊，以盡快適應移民愛爾蘭後嘅新生活。呢個移民講座由愛爾蘭政府資助，我哋打算喺九月底 (九月三十日星期六)舉行呢個活動。宜家我哋希望你可以幫幫手填份問卷，等我哋知道你哋需要乜嘢資料。
Fill out the survey 填問卷

Survey: Online Cantonese Course
問卷調查︰網上粵語課程


Cantonese in Ireland is planning to organise another Cantonese course for interested people. Please help to fill the survey so we can design and deliver the course that meets the needs of the public. 

At this stage, in our plan, our course will be online, and it will be theme-based rather than level-based, so all levels of learners can join.

<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>亦打算為對廣東話有興趣嘅人開辦網上粵語課程。請幫忙填吓問卷，好讓我哋根據大眾嘅需要設計及安排課程。

現階段，我哋構思嘅粵語課程會係以題目主導，希望所有不同粵語程度嘅同學都可以一齊上堂。


If you want tailor-made one to one Cantonese classes, online or in person, you can also email us: hello@cantoneseireland.ie, to discuss in details.

如果你希望我哋為你度身訂造提供一對一面授或網上教學，你可以電郵我哋︰hello@cantoneseireland.ie。

Heritage Week Bilingual Storytelling

民族文化遺產週粵英雙語講故事


We had great time in Cork City Library and also Mitchelstown Library. With the Irish public, we learned the Cantonese number one to ten, sang the classic number song, listened to the story about our Irish Goddess and Saint Brigid, and also made Brigid's Crosses together.

You can now download our presentation here.

我哋呢兩個星期民族文化遺產週喺Cork City 圖書館同埋Mitchelstown圖書館玩得好開心。我哋同愛爾蘭大眾一齊學粵語數目字一到十，又一齊唱經典兒歌數字歌，仲聽埋愛爾蘭女神同聖人嘅故事，做埋St. Brigid's 十字架。

你可以喺度下載我哋嘅故仔。

hello@cantoneseireland.ie

As you can see from this newsletter, we have worked very hard to promote Cantonese culture in Ireland and support Cantonese migrants to integrate into Irish society. Since our start in 2022, all the work has been done by volunteers. Even though we have a small amount of public funds, our regular expenses rely on your support. If you want to see our work continue to contribute to Ireland, please help us so that we can continue this important work. 

大家都睇到，我哋不斷努力致力於愛爾蘭推動粵語語言及文化，亦希望幫助粵語移民融入愛爾蘭生活。本會成立以嚟，單靠義工及各善長人翁出錢出力，雖然我哋開始有政府及其他機構嘅贊助，但我哋嘅年度開支，都仍然須靠大眾嘅捐款支持。如果你想見到我哋繼續係本地付出，請各位多多支持，等大家可以繼續一齊走落去。
