Cantonese Wedding Online Workshop
廣東婚嫁兩語講座

Wanna know what the traditions and etiquettes of Cantonese Weddings are? How does the traditional Cantonese wedding dress look like? What is the use of the umbrella and the rice? Why do the couple kneel down in front of the parents? What? A wedding chaperone...?

What are all the must-do steps of these complex big events, and why is it done like that?

Join our online event hosted by Cantonese in Ireland and Wayne Chau to find out more!

(Event funded by Dublin Bus Community Spirit Award)

Coffee, Chat & Register to vote [Dublin]

On 13th of May, join us for a meaningful afternoon of free coffee and chit-chats while filling your registration form to vote!

Dim Sum, Chat& Register to vote [Cork]

On 13th of May, join us for a meaningful afternoon of dim sum and chit-chats while filling your registration form to vote!

The meet-up will be at Yuan Ming Yuan at 12.30pm.

You just need to pay for 10euro and we will cover the rest. You can then taste all these different delicious, delicate Cantonese dim sum dishes. All welcome, especially immigrants. Children aged below 12 are free to join.

This is a follow-up event after the Migrant Political Participation Workshop. But you don’t need to have attended the workshop to join this dim sum meet-up.

To support Cantonest in Ireland Team - Cheung Po Tsai

For the first time ever, we have our own team participating at the Irish Dragon Boat Regatta. We have named our team after the famous Cantonese pirate king Cheung Po Tsai. 

Come and support our team while snaclking on some free dumplings!

Venue: Carlow Town Park

Time to be confirmed (around 10-5pm)

Accessible by bus or by car. Details to meet to follow.

    As you can see from this newsletter, we have worked very hard to promote Cantonese culture in Ireland and support Cantonese migrants to integrate into Irish society. Since our start in 2022, all the work has been done by volunteers. Even though we have a small amount of public funds, our regular expenses rely on your support. If you want to see our work continue to contribute to Ireland, please help us so that we can continue this important work. 

    大家都睇到，我哋不斷努力致力於愛爾蘭推動粵語語言及文化，亦希望幫助粵語移民融入愛爾蘭生活。本會成立以嚟，單靠義工及各善長人翁出錢出力，雖然我哋開始有政府及其他機構嘅贊助，但我哋嘅年度開支，都仍然須靠大眾嘅捐款支持。如果你想見到我哋繼續係本地付出，請各位多多支持，等大家可以繼續一齊走落去。
