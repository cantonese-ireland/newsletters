Dear Cantonese lovers,

各位親愛嘅粵語愛好者︰

In the aftermath of the series of events in Dublin last Thursday, we must reinforce the importance of spreading kindness in our community. Many of us at Cantonese in Ireland are immigrants, and we know it is never easy. Reach out if you need help.

Stay safe and take good care of yourself. If you can, take care of those around you too.

If you experience or witness racism, you can reach out to the Irish Network Against Racism via https://inar.ie/ireport-reporting-centres/

You can report hate crime via https://www.garda.ie/en/reportahatecrime/ or phone 01 666 3150. If you would like to talk to someone about it, you can contact the Crime Victims Helpline for free at 116 006.

You can also get the latest update for public transport here: https://www.transportforireland.ie/news/travel-disruptions-in-dublin/

經過上星期四喺都柏林發生嘅一系列事件後，我哋必須強調喺社區中傳播善意既重要性。我哋粵語愛爾蘭入面，有好多成員都係移民，我哋知道喺外地生活絕非易事。

如果你哋需要協助，請聯絡我哋。希望大家能夠注意安全，如果可以嘅話，也請多關心身邊既人。

如果你正經歷或者目睹種族歧視，你可以透過 https://inar.ie/ireport-reporting-centres/ 聯繫相關部門。

你亦可以透過 https://www.garda.ie/en/reportahatecrime/ 或致電 01 666 3150 向警方舉報仇恨犯罪行為。如果你想同人傾吓呢件事，可以撥打 116 006 免費幫助熱線。

另外，有關最新交通消息狀況，可以瀏覽︰https://www.transportforireland.ie/news/travel-disruptions-in-dublin/

As you can see from this newsletter, we have worked very hard to promote Cantonese culture in Ireland and support Cantonese migrants to integrate into Irish society. Since our start in 2022, all the work has been done by volunteers. Even though we have a small amount of public funds, our regular expenses rely on your support. If you want to see our work continue to contribute to Ireland, please help us so that we can continue this important work. 

大家都睇到，我哋不斷努力致力於愛爾蘭推動粵語語言及文化，亦希望幫助粵語移民融入愛爾蘭生活。本會成立以嚟，單靠義工及各善長人翁出錢出力，雖然我哋開始有政府及其他機構嘅贊助，但我哋嘅年度開支，都仍然須靠大眾嘅捐款支持。如果你想見到我哋繼續係本地付出，請各位多多支持，等大家可以繼續一齊走落去。
