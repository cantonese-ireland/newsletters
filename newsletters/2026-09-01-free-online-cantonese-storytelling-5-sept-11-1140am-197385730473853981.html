🏮🥮 The Mid-Autumn Festival is coming! 🥮🏮

For the Chinese and all others who celebrate, it is customary to carry lanterns and eat mooncakes with your family. The mooncakes' round shape symbolises completedness and reunion within the family.

Join us this Saturday for a cosy online bilingual storytelling session in Cantonese and English featuring Kam Chai and the Old Bakery by Ms Liv.

This delightful story is written in Cantonese (Jyutman/粵文) with Jyutping and English translation, making it perfect for both Cantonese-speaking families and children learning the language.

We're also delighted to welcome Kiki, an experienced preschool teacher, as our storyteller this weekend. With her warm and engaging storytelling style, Kiki will bring the story to life and encourage children to join in the fun.

Please prepare coloring pens 🖍️, square paper 📜 and scissors ✂️ for your children!

🏮🥮 中秋節就快來臨啦！ 🥮🏮

呢個節日不可缺乏嘅傳統，當然就係點燈籠同埋食月餅啦！月餅嘅形狀圓揼揼，象徵一家團圓、合一同埋完整無缺，非常有意義㗎！

今個星期六，我哋誠邀大家參加粵英雙語網上故事時間，一齊欣賞 Ms Liv 嘅《金仔同埋老餅家》（Kam Chai and the Old Bakery）。

呢本充滿溫暖同智慧嘅繪本，以粵文（Cantonese/Jyutman）書寫，並附有粵拼（Jyutping）及英文翻譯，非常適合粵家庭同正喺度學習粵語嘅小朋友。

今次我哋好高興仲邀請到擁有豐富幼兒教育經驗嘅 Kiki老師 擔任故事姐姐！Kiki老師會用生動有趣嘅方式帶領小朋友進入故事世界，一齊互動、分享同享受閱讀樂趣。

請各位家長為小朋友準備彩色筆🖍️、方形紙📜同埋鉸剪✂️！
