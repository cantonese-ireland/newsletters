Cantonese movie!

Thrilled to know that the Cantonese movie Twilight of the Warriors: Walled In will be up on Odeon in Point Square, Dublin 1 from 25th May. The tickets are not being sold yet, but do remember the date. 

Kowloon Wall City is famous around the world, although it's abolished in Hong Kong, the tales never dies. The movie is entertaining and the reconstruction of Kowloon Wall City is phenomenal. Of course, there is the Kung Fu that you can't miss. 


港產片呀！

好高興得知粵語電影《九龍城寨之圍牆》將於 5 月 25 日起喺都柏林 一區Point Square 嘅 Odeon 上映。雖然門票尚未發售，但請記住呢個日子。

九龍城寨聞名於世，雖然城寨建築已經消失，但城寨傳說卻繼續流傳。呢部電影非常有趣，場景重建都似得驚人。當然，仲有你不能錯過嘅香港武打。
Film Details 電影詳情

As you can see from this newsletter, we have worked very hard to promote Cantonese culture in Ireland and support Cantonese migrants to integrate into Irish society. Since our start in 2022, all the work has been done by volunteers. If you want to see our work continue to contribute to Ireland, please help us so that we can continue this important work. 

大家都睇到，我哋不斷努力致力於愛爾蘭推動粵語語言及文化，亦希望幫助粵語移民融入愛爾蘭生活。本會成立以嚟，單靠義工及各善長人翁出錢出力。如果你想見到我哋繼續係本地付出，請各位多多支持，等大家可以繼續一齊走落去。
