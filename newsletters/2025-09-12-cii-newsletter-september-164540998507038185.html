Greetings from Cantonese in Ireland

As summer draws to a close and we step into the crisp, colourful days of autumn, many of us are settling back into school, college, and new routines.

This change of season is the perfect time to reflect on the meaningful activities we enjoyed over the summer—and to look forward with excitement to what’s coming next.

Over the past few months, our community has been active and engaged:

  • Sharing Cantonese culture through creative workshops

  • Bringing joy and care to elderly residents in Dublin

  • Celebrating heritage and traditions with both old and new friends

  • Building bridges between cultures here in Ireland

We are deeply grateful to all the volunteers, supporters, and participants who made these projects possible. Your energy and dedication are what keep our community vibrant and strong.

As we move into autumn, we’re excited to bring you a fresh line-up of events—including talks, tours, and cultural celebrations—that will continue to highlight the richness of Cantonese language and heritage in Ireland.

🍂 This autumn, we invite you to:

  • Attend engaging talks and cultural presentation

  • Connect with others—both online and in person—as part of our growing Cantonese-speaking community in Ireland

📣 Stay connected, get involved, and let’s continue building this wonderful community together!

History of Cantonese Diaspora in Ireland

Long before the Celtic Tiger immigration boom, the first Cantonese migrants, mostly from Hong Kong, arrived in Ireland in the late 1950s and early 1960s. Over the decades, their journey has evolved: from pioneers in catering businesses, to international students, to today’s professionals and second-generation Cantonese whose home is Ireland.

This online talk will explore the experiences, challenges, and contributions of the Cantonese-speaking community in Ireland. Hear personal stories, cultural insights, and academic perspectives from Alice Chau, producer and presenter of the Asian Talents radio show on NearFM.

Whether you’re part of the diaspora, interested in multicultural studies, or simply curious about the Cantonese community’s impact in Ireland, this event will be both educational and inspiring.

Lecture will be conducted in English
This event forms part of the Dublin Festival of History.

粵裔愛爾蘭 歷史新尋

喺「凱爾特之虎」經濟起飛之前，第一批粵裔移民（多數嚟自香港）已經喺1950年代末至1960年代初移居愛爾蘭。經過幾十年，粵裔社群嘅面貌不斷變化：由最初經營餐飲業，到留學生、專業人士，以至喺愛爾蘭土生土長嘅第二代，都成為愛爾蘭社會嘅重要一部分。

呢個網上講座，講者 Alice Chau（NearFM《亞洲愛才》節目主持人及製作人）會透過分享個人故事、文化同學術見解，同大家探索粵語社群嘅經歷、挑戰同貢獻。

無論你係粵裔、對多元文化有興趣，或者想了解粵裔對愛爾蘭嘅影響，都唔可以錯過！

Meet Tindy our qualified Accountant and Tax advisor

Hello, my name is Tindy and I am originally from Hong Kong. I have been on and off living in Ireland more than 15 years now. I work as a qualified Accountant and Tax advisor in Dublin. Prior to that, I obtained a registered nursing license which specializes in NICU (Neonatal Intensive Care) in Hong Kong. I worked in a private hospital for a bit longer than a year before I moved back to Dublin permanently.


你好，我叫 Tindy ，我係香港人。我喺愛爾蘭斷斷續續噉住咗超過15年。我喺都柏林係一個合格嘅會計師同稅務顧問。 之前，我攞咗個註冊護理牌照，係香港專門做 NICU （新生兒深切治療）。 我喺一間私家醫院做咗一年多少少，之後我就永久搬返都柏林。

There was a social media post that drawn my attention long time ago. A lady fought very hard that Traditional Chinese language to be accepted and recognised by leaving Cert examination in Ireland. I remember I signed the petition as well. It struck me. So when Cantonese Ireland is born, I have followed them since. I formally joined them about 2 years ago, a committee member invites me to join them as they need a hand for handling their financial and compliance obligation. It was great to be able to use my strength to help them.

好耐以前，有個社交媒體帖子引起咗我嘅注意。一位女士好努力噉爭取繁體漢子喺愛爾蘭嘅公開考試中被接受同認可。我記得當時我都簽咗份請願書。 嗰件事解動咗我，所以當粵語愛爾蘭出世嘅時候，我就追蹤佢哋嘅社交媒體。大約兩年前左右，有個委員會成員邀請我加入佢哋，因為佢哋需要人幫手處理佢哋嘅財務及其他各項事務。可以用我嘅知識去幫佢哋真係好正。

My main responsibility is to handle their financial and compliance obligation. I have given a talk about the taxation in Ireland for some new immigrant before and I occasionally will join other committees to participate some events as well.

我嘅主要責任係處理佢哋嘅財務及稅務事宜。我之前主講愛爾蘭稅務講座，幫助新移民對愛爾蘭交稅事宜。偶爾我都會同其他委員會一齊搞活動。

I hope the Cantonese immigrants will not feel lonely when they move to a foreign country by knowing that they can find brunch of people who can speak the same language with them, probably will have a same goal to preserve our own cultural heritage as well.

我希望粵移民喺佢哋搬去外國嗰陣唔會覺得寂寞，因為佢哋知道自己可以搵到一堆人可以同佢哋講同一種語言，可能仲會有同樣嘅目標去保存我哋自己嘅文化遺產。
