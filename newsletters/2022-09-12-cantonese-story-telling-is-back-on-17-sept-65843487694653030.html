Dear parents, 

各位親愛的家長︰


We hope you all had a great summer and survive back to school! Here are some kids-friendly events that are coming up, and thought you might be interested to know. 

你們的暑假愉快嗎？希望孩子們都順利回到校園。以下是一些即將舉辦，而又適合一家大細一齊參與的活動，或者你會感興趣。

Cantonese Stories on Saturdays 週六廣東話故事時間

Cantonese Stories on Saturdays is back. We have Queenie, Sally and Heidi joining as storytellers in our team.

On every 1st and 3rd Saturday of each month, we will have online storytelling section.  

On every 2nd Saturday of each month, if you want to meet up in person in Cork City Library, do come.

The upcoming Storytelling will be on 17th September, happening online. 

週六廣東話故事時間又回來！今個學年，我們有三位出色講故人加入，跟我們講故事，她們就是Queenie, Sally及Heidi。

逢第一及第三個週六早上，我們都會於網上講故事。

逢第二個週六早上呢，故事時間將於高基巿圖書館進行。

九月十七日星期六早上，網上故事時間正式開始。
Book now! 訂票

Cork Culture Night 高基文化夜

In Cork, we will have a Bilingual Storytelling in Cantonese and English in Cork City Library. The legend of Soeng Ngo (嫦娥) flying to the moon will be told following a simple lantern making for the kiddies and the biggies.

The Storytelling will be on 23rd September, starting at 4.15pm.

在高基，我們將會在高基巿圖書館以廣東話英語雙語，講出嫦娥奔月的傳統中秋節故事。然後會做一個簡單的小燈籠。

故事時間將於九月二十三日 (週五)，下午四時十五分開始。

Social Meetups on Saturdays 週六聚一聚 

From October, we will have Cantonese Social Meetups every Saturday afternoon at 2.30pm. Everyone is welcome. Are you looking for opportunities to speak Cantonese? Do you want to meet more people? Come meet up at CHQ in Dublin. 

由十月開始，我們會有廣東話聚一聚。你想講廣東話？你想識新朋友？來參加我們每月第一個星期六下午二時半的聚會啦。每個月第一個星期六，都柏林巿中心，CHQ見你喇！

We need your help to continue these programmes

As you have noticed, all the educational programmes above are FREE and done by volunteers using their own time, effort and expertise. However, we do have some regular expenses to keep the organisation running. If you have enjoyed these free events and would love to see us continue our work, please donate if you can. Even €5 is helpful. And if you can sign up to monthly contribution it's even better. 

你可能已經注意到，上述所有教育計劃都是免費的，由志願者利用他們自己的時間、精力和專業知識完成。但是，我們確實需要資金保持組織的常規運作。如果你喜歡這些免費活動，並希望我們可以繼續我們的工作，請捐款。即使是 5 歐元也會對我們很有幫助。如果你能註冊每月供款，那當然更好。
