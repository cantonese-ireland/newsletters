Free Irish lesson 免費愛爾蘭語課程

Funded by Department of Children, Equality, Disability, Integration and Youth 

由愛爾蘭政府兒童青年平等機會及傷健局贊助


Can't read the Irish road signs? Can't pronounce Siobhan, Micheál, Tadhg or Gráinne? Do not get what the craic is when people are laughing? Cannot help with your own child's Irish homework?

Now here is the chance!

We are honoured to have Dr. Qiu from University College Dublin (UCD) to design and deliver this Beginner Irish Language Course for all who speak Cantonese.

The cost? FREE, FREE, FREE! (Speak thrice for important matters).

And you can take away CEFR A1 level in Irish when the course ends.
