Mission Accomplished 工作報告

Great success of Lunar New Year celebrations

Dublin Lunar New Year Festival has been a great success. We have organised a record-breaking number of events and have served the community. 

  • Online Bilingual Storytelling x 2
  • Library Bilingual Storytelling x 3 (Cork City library, Lexicon Library)
  • Movie & Coffee Meetup: Comrades, Almost a Love Story
  • Lunar New Year Lunch in Cork
  • Dublin Lunar New Year Flagship event: Shamrock knot & Fish Prawn Crab
  • Online Cantonese Taster Class
  • Dim Sum Lunch with migrant supports

These events not only have we connected with public and private sectors big and small in Ireland, the most importantly, we have sparked the connection with Cantonese migrants in Ireland. Not only we managed to promote the Cantonese language and culture to the Irish public, but we also demonstrated to the Irish public that our organisation is not only for migrants; we are here to serve Ireland. 


農曆新年活動經已完滿結束

都柏林農曆新年慶典非常成功。我哋舉辦活動數量創新高，為本地社區服務。

  • 線上雙語講故事 x 2
  • 圖書館雙語講故事 x 3（高基市圖書館，Lexicon圖書館）
  • 電影及咖啡聚：甜蜜蜜
  • 高基農曆新年午餐
  • 都柏林農曆新年旗艦活動：三葉草結。魚蝦蟹
  • 網上粵語體驗班
  • 支持移民友人點心午餐

共服務 489 人次， 345 非粵語為母語人士。

呢啲活動，令愛爾蘭本地各大小機構、公營私營，都認識到<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>。更重要嘅係，大眾認識到一班喺愛爾蘭嘅廣東話移民。我哋唔單止將廣東話、廣東文化傳揚開去，仲建立咗大家對我哋嘅認同，令大家都知道我哋組織唔單止服務粵語移民，我哋一班廣東話移民仲係一班會關心同埋肯參與愛爾蘭發展嘅自己人。

As you can see from this newsletter, we have worked very hard to promote Cantonese culture in Ireland and support Cantonese migrants to integrate into Irish society. Since our start in 2022, all the work has been done by volunteers. Even though we have a small amount of public funds, our regular expenses rely on your support. If you want to see our work continue to contribute to Ireland, please help us so that we can continue this important work. 

大家都睇到，我哋不斷努力致力於愛爾蘭推動粵語語言及文化，亦希望幫助粵語移民融入愛爾蘭生活。本會成立以嚟，單靠義工及各善長人翁出錢出力，雖然我哋開始有政府及其他機構嘅贊助，但我哋嘅年度開支，都仍然須靠大眾嘅捐款支持。如果你想見到我哋繼續係本地付出，請各位多多支持，等大家可以繼續一齊走落去。
