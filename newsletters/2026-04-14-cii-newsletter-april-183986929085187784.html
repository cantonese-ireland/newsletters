Dear Cantonese lovers,

各位親愛嘅粵語愛好者︰

Do you know, readers, there’s an old Irish belief called the “Borrowed Days,” when April borrows a few days from March to bring one last burst of wild weather. People used to say these days could “skin a cow,” not literally, but to show how harsh the cold winds and rain could be. It might explain why early April still brings strong winds, cold snaps, and even flight disruptions in Dublin.

各位讀者，你哋知唔知，愛爾蘭傳統有一個說法，認為四月會向三月借幾日，帶來最後一波惡劣天氣。以前有人話呢幾日可以「剝走牛皮」，形容天氣又凍又濕又大風，非常惡劣。諗落，終於知道點解我呢四月初會有強風、氣溫驟降，甚至連都柏林嘅航班都受影響。

Celebrated Cantonese Culture at St. Patrick’s Day in Dún Laoghaire! ☘️

Cantonese in Ireland was honoured to take part in this year’s St. Patrick’s Day parade in Dún Laoghaire, bringing a vibrant splash of Cantonese culture to the streets!

Our volunteers delighted crowds with traditional windmills imported from Hong Kong and the lively sounds of Cantonese music resonating along the parade route. Walking alongside diverse community groups, we were proud to showcase our heritage as part of Ireland’s rich multicultural community.

We’re grateful for the warm welcome and support from the public, fellow participants, and organizers. Your enthusiasm reminds us why cultural exchange and community connection are so important.

Thanks to our amazing volunteers and everyone cheering us on, it’s a privilege to share our culture and be part of this celebration. We can’t wait for next year! 💚

Our Volunteers 我哋嘅義工

In this issue, we would like to introduce you to our volunteer Grace!
呢期通訊，我哋想同大家介紹我哋嘅義工Grace！

Tell us something about yourself

I am a linguist specializing in speech, forensic phonetics and trilingualism. Growing up in Cantonese culture, Cantonese identity has always been one of my most important identities.

自我介紹

Hello，我係 Grace，我係一位喺大學教書嘅語言學家。嚟愛爾蘭之前，我喺香港做開關於粵語、普通話同埋英文呢三種語言之間點樣互相影響嘅研究。嚟到愛爾蘭之後，我都希望有機會可以繼續做多啲同粵語相關嘅研究。

我喺一個充滿粵語文化嘅環境下長大，由細到大都係睇 TVB 劇集、聽廣東歌、飲茶，仲好鍾意食燒味同煲仔飯。「粵語人」呢個身份，係我咁多個身份之中嘅一個好重要嘅身份認同。佢唔單止影響住我對公共事務嘅參與，仲深深影響我嘅研究、我嘅思考方式，以及我同人之間嘅連結。

What brought you to Ireland?

I received a job offer from Dublin, therefore I packed all of my stuff into two luggages, left Hong Kong and came to Ireland. As I studied in the UK before, Ireland is similar to the UK in some sense, but the longer I stay here, the more uniqueness of Irish culture that I discover. I feel lucky that I have the opportunity to work and live in such an amazing country, and contribute my part to the society through teaching and research.

乜嘢令你嚟到愛爾蘭？

我同愛爾蘭嘅故事，係由一個 job offer 開始嘅。嗰陣我將生存所需嘅物資同埋書塞晒入兩個行李箱，就咁樣飛咗嚟愛爾蘭。因為之前喺英國讀過書，愛爾蘭對我嚟講唔算太陌生，始終呢度嘅教育制度同文化同英國都有好多相似之處。但係喺愛爾蘭留得越耐，我就越發現呢個地方獨特同埋可愛之處。我覺得自己好幸運，可以喺一個咁靚嘅國家工作同生活。我都希望可以透過我嘅教書同研究，去回饋返愛爾蘭社會。

How did you start with Cantonese in Ireland?

When I arrived Dublin, I started to look for Cantonese community. Cantonese in Ireland poped up in my searching. The first event I went to was St. Patrick's Day parade in Dun Laoghaire, that was a lot of fun. I met so many new friends there. 


你係點樣喺CII開始㗎？

    我嚟到都柏林之後，就開始喺網上搵下有無粵語人社群，而 CII 係我第一個搵到嘅機構。第一次參加 CII 嘅活動係舊年聖博德節（St. Patrick's Day）嘅巡遊，嗰次活動真係好好玩，我亦都喺嗰度識咗好多新朋友。

    What have you done with Cantonese in Ireland?

    I organised several events, such as invited a professor who works on Hong Kong Hakka to Dublin and talked about heritage language; I helped with the Lunar New Year event of calligraphy drawing; I also hosted a book talk of Time Difference and the writer Ki. 

    你同CII做過啲咩？
    我組織過幾場活動，例如舊年 9 月我請咗一位做香港客家話研究嘅教授嚟都柏林開講座，講關於heritage language嘅課題。今年農曆新年寫揮春嘅活動我都有幫手。而今年復活節，我亦都主持咗一個新書分享會，香港作家 Ki 帶埋佢本著作《時差》嚟到都柏林，同我哋分享佢喺愛爾蘭讀書嘅點點滴滴同感悟。

    Hope for Cantonese immigrants in ireland

      As a linguist, my hope is definitely language-related. I hope Cantonese can pass on to more younger generation in Ireland. I also hope that more people are involved in building the Cantonese community in Ireland. 

      對喺愛爾蘭嘅廣東人嘅願望

      作為一個語言學家，我嘅願望自然係同語言有關嘅。我希望有更多家庭可以將粵語傳承落去，等更多後生仔喺愛爾蘭繼續講廣東話。我都希望大家可以積極參與，一齊共建愛爾蘭嘅粵語人社群。

      📢 Traditional Chinese not recognised in Leaving Cert Mandarin — Background + Call for Support from LCMC Concern Group

      A quick background for those who may not be familiar:

      In 2021, it was discovered that the Leaving Certificate Mandarin Chinese exam (Ireland’s university entrance exam) only accepted simplified Chinese as correct answers, and would mark other forms (including traditional Chinese) as wrong.

      Currently, Mandarin is mainly taught and examined by native Chinese teachers, most of whom can read both simplified and traditional Chinese. This means allowing traditional Chinese would not create additional financial or administrative burden.

      In fact, members of the traditional Chinese community in Ireland have offered to support the government voluntarily.

      Because of this issue, LCMC Concern Group – Ireland was formed:

      👉 Join : [https://www.facebook.com/groups/lcmccon/]

      📢 They now need your help

      A formal review is open till 17th April (Friday) — this is a chance to officially voice that penalising traditional Chinese is unfair.

      👉 Takes ~20 mins:

      ✔ Anyone can fill it in (any country / any role)

      ✔ Just write “concerned person” in Participant Details

      It’s ok to answer “don’t know” / ‘no opinions’ — main thing is to say (in the sections of written / assessment/ other comments :

      * It’s unfair to penalise traditional Chinese

      * It goes against plurilingual & cultural diversity

      * Both systems should be treated equally


      📢 嚟自愛爾蘭高考華文科關注組嘅呼籲

      簡單背景：

      2021年，愛爾蘭高考正式開設華文科，但考試只接受簡體字為正確答案，其他寫法（包括繁體字）會被視為錯誤。

      目前教授及評核華文科嘅多係以華語為母語為嘅教師，佢哋都能閱讀簡體及繁體字，因此允許使用繁體字唔會增加額外成本或行政負擔。

      此外，喺愛爾蘭嘅繁體社群亦曾主動提出義務協助政府。

      愛爾蘭高考華文科關注組就係喺呢個背景下成立。
