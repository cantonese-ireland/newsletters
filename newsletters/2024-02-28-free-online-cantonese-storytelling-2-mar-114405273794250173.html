Schreimutter is story written by German author Jutta Bauer. In German, 'Schrei' means shout and 'mutter' means mother.

Little Penguin is broken apart because of the scream from Mammy Penguin. What will Mammy Penguin do?

《Schreimutter 大吼大叫的企鵝媽媽》是德國作家Jutta Bauer寫嘅故事。 Schrei 嘅意思係「吼叫」，mutter 指「母親」。

小企鵝因為企鵝媽媽嘅大吼大叫而嚇到身體各部份都飛散世界各地。企鵝媽媽可以做點做呢？
