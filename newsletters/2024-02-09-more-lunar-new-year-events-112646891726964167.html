10th Feb: Cork City Library Bilingual Storytelling

二月十號︰高基市圖書館雙語故事時間

10th Feb is the first day of our Lunar New Year! And this is the year of Dragon. Come join us in Cork City Library to find out more about this magical animal of the East at our Bilingual Storytelling (Canto & Eng) from 2-2.40pm this Saturday 10th Feb. 2月10日係我哋農曆新年嘅第一日！今年係龍年。歡迎嚟到高基市圖書館參加我哋粵英雙語故事時間，了解一吓我哋嘅東方神獸。 2 月 10 號星期六，下午 2 點到2 點 40 分，約定你！講完故事後我們將做一個同龍有關嘅簡單藝術作品，仲會一齊學寫「龍」呢個繁體漢字。今個星期六，我哋一齊慶祝農曆新年啦！

二月十一號︰都柏林農曆新年慶典- 食住上︰麻雀 X 鬥獸棋

15th Feb: Miniature Flowerboard Workshop
二月十五號︰迷你花牌工作坊

Flowerboard crafting technique is listed as Hong Kong Intangible Cultural Heritage. It is used in different celebrating occasions in Hong Kong: shop openings, concerts or religious festivals.

To celebrate the Lunar New Year of Dragon, Cantonese in Ireland would is going to introduce this beautiful flowerboard to our audience in Mitchelstown Library.

In this Miniature Flowerboard Workshop, participants will explore the design of flowerboards. Participants will use cardboards to make the base, detailed with a printout of dragon, phoenix and cloud patterns. Red clothes and pre-made craft flowers will be used. Everyone will get the hands-on experience to make a miniature flower board and bring home.

Date: 15th February 2024 (Thursday)
Time: 11.30-12.30am
Venue: Mitchelstown Library

花牌製作技藝被列為香港非物質文化遺產。喺香港不同慶祝場合都會見到：商店開幕、音樂會或宗教節日。

為了慶祝農曆龍年，粵語愛爾蘭人將會喺Mitchelstown Library同大家介紹呢個美麗嘅民間工藝。

喺呢個迷你花牌工作坊中，參與者將會探索花牌嘅設計。我哋會用紙板製作底座，並列龍、鳳、雲嘅圖案，亦會用紅布同預先製作嘅工藝花裝飾花牌。完咗之後，大家都可以將迷你花牌帶返屋企。

日期：2024年2月15日（星期四）
時間：11.30-12.30am
地點：Mitchelstown Library
