Mid Autumn Lantern Making Workshop

中秋燈籠工作坊

Mid-Autumn Festival is a harvest festival. Through this workshop, you will get to taste the different mooncakes, make the lanterns, know the legends and learn some Cantonese. 

中秋節是一個慶祝豐收的傳統節日。透過我們的工作坊，你得以品嚐不同月餅、聽傳統故事，更可以帶走兩個燈籠。

Last Saturday we had our first-time ever Cantonese+English storytelling in Cork City library. We will be doing the bilingual storytelling there once a month. You will learn Cantonese through stories and Arts and Crafts.

上週六，我們在CorkCity library舉辦了在愛爾蘭圖書館歷史上首次的粵語+英語雙語故事分享活動。從今以後，我們將會每月一次在CorkCity library粵語+英語向各位大小朋友講故事。透過故事及手工等，我們一起學習粵語。

Another free event we have with National Heritage Week is happening this Saturday: Cork City Walking Tour in English and Cantonese. 

另一個和國家民族遺產週合作的活動將會在今個星期六舉行。屆時我們會以粵語英語認識高基巿中心的一些文物。

Introducing 隆重介紹
Donation page 籌款專頁

Cantonese in Ireland is a registered non-profit organisation working to promote Cantonese culture in Ireland and support Cantonese migrants to integrate into Irish society. Since our start in 2022, all the work has been done by volunteers. However, we do have some regular expenses to keep the organisation running. Your support will make a big difference to us.

粵語愛爾蘭為已註冊非牟利組織，致力於愛爾蘭推動粵語語言及文化，亦希望幫助粵語融入愛爾蘭生活。本會成立以來單靠義工及各善長人翁出錢出力，下半年開支仍須靠大眾的捐款支持。
