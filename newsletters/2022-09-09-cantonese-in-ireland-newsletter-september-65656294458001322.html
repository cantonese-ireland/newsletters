Dear Cantonese lovers, 

各位親愛的粵語愛好者︰


Can you believe it? In less than a year, Cantonese in Ireland has organised 23 events! We have organised these events out of our passion and love to our Cantonese language and Irish community. In these coming months, we would keep on providing you different events. 

在短短一年不夠，我們 <<粵語。愛爾蘭>> 已經舉辦了二十三個活動，真是不可思議！舉辦這些活動，全因為熱愛我們的廣東文化及愛爾蘭。在未來數月，我們仍然會為大家送上不同的活動。

 

Culture Night 文化夜

We have joined Culture Night this year and will be having events in Dublin and Cork on 23rd September. 

今年9月23日愛爾蘭文化夜，我們將會於為大家呈上兩項活動。一項在都柏林，另一項在高基。

Cork Culture Night 高基文化夜

In Cork, we will have a Bilingual Storytelling in Cantonese and English in Cork City Library. The legend of Soeng Ngo (嫦娥) flying to the moon will be told following a simple lantern making for the kiddies and the biggies. 

Click for details 詳情

在高基，我們將會在高基巿圖書館以廣東話英語雙語，講出嫦娥奔月的傳統中秋節故事。然後會做一個簡單的小燈籠。

Dublin Culture Night 都柏林文化夜

In Dublin, we will have a walking tour starting from 4pm.

The tour is for free. Cantonese related arts in the city will be shown. And of course, we can't miss the yummy Cantonese snacks. The booking is full in less than a day. 

Click for details 詳情

在都柏林，我們將會於四時開始我們的都柏林巿徒步遊。我們會帶大家發掘都柏林巿的廣東文化面，當然還要帶大家會去品嚐美味點心啦。徒步遊免費參加，可惜已經額滿。

Social Meetups on Saturdays 週六聚一聚 

From October, we will have Cantonese Social Meetups every Saturday afternoon at 2.30pm. Everyone is welcome. Are you looking for opportunities to speak Cantonese? Do you want to meet more people? Come meet up at CHQ in Dublin. 

由十月開始，我們缃會有廣東話聚一聚。你想講廣東話？你想識新朋友？來參加我們每月第一個星期六下午二時半的聚會啦。每個月第一個星期六，都柏林巿中心，CHQ見你喇！

Cantonese Stories on Saturdays 週六廣東話故事時間

Cantonese Stories on Saturdays is back. We have Queenie, Sally and Heidi joining as storytellers in our team.

On every 1st and 3rd Saturday of each month, we will have online storytelling section.  

On every 2nd Saturday of each month, if you want to meet up in person in Cork City Library, do come.

The upcoming Storytelling will be on 17th September, happening online. 

週六廣東話故事時間又回來！今個學年，我們有三位出色講故人加入，跟我們講故事，她們是Queenie, Sally及Heidi。

逢第一及第三個週六早上，我們都會於網上講故事。

逢第二個週六早上呢，故事時間將於高基巿圖書館進行。

九月十七日星期六早上，網上故事時間正式開始。

Childhood Photos Wanted 誠徵童年照

We will have an online exhibition with The Museum of Childhood Ireland. Date to be confirmed.

We will be showing the childhood photos of our Cantonese Irish. Any photos of you in the park? Any photos of you playing fun games? Or you in the takeaway messing around/ working away? Or you in your home with your parents? Relatives visiting? Photos showing your favourite past-times? Favourite Cantonese books/ comic/ magazines? Or simply a photo of you smiling in the cloudy day...?

Email us your photos: cantonese.ireland@gmail.com

我們將會跟愛爾蘭兒童博物館合作，舉辦網上相片展覽，展出於愛爾蘭成長的廣東話朋友的童年照片。你有沒有一些珍貴的相片，或者是你在公園玩的相片？或者是你在外賣店攪亂檔 / 努力工作中？你和父母的合照？親戚到訪愛爾蘭？你最喜歡的兒時玩意？粵文圖書/漫畫/雜誌？或者簡單一幅你在陰天時仍笑得燦爛的相片？

請將你的相片連同簡單幾句，電郵到: cantonese.ireland@gmail.com
Donation

Cantonese in Ireland is a registered non-profit organisation working to promote Cantonese culture in Ireland and support Cantonese migrants to integrate into Irish society. Since our start in 2022, all the work has been done by volunteers. However, we do have some regular expenses to keep the organisation running. Your support will make a big difference to us.

粵語愛爾蘭為已註冊非牟利組織，致力於愛爾蘭推動粵語語言及文化，亦希望幫助粵語移民融入愛爾蘭生活。本會成立以來單靠義工及各善長人翁出錢出力，下半年開支仍須靠大眾的捐款支持。
