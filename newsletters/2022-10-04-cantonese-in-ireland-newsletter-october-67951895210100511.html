Our first social meetup 第一次「週六聚一聚」

Our first social meetup was a success! We had people from Hong Kong, UK, Macau, Malaysia, and an Irish person with Hongkongese heritage looking to reconnect with their roots. If you miss it yesterday, join us next time!

第一次「週六聚一聚」已經成功完成， 在場不單止有嚟自香港、英國、澳門同馬來西亞嘅朋友，亦有愛爾蘭香港人後裔嚟尋根，希望可以學吓廣東話同上一代溝通。錯過咗嘅朋友，下次記得嚟喇！

Event: Irish history and pub culture night

Date: 21-10-2022 (Friday)

Time: 8 to 10pm

Venue: The Cobblestones (77 King St N, Smithfield, Dublin, D07 TP22)

Fee: €15 per person (snacks included but not drinks)

Details: Immerse into the world famous Irish pub culture and learn Irish history while having fun - quiz, poetry, drinking songs, an authentic Irish experience to help you integrate to the local culture.

Host: Dave Lordan, a renowned Irish poet, historian, writer, teacher, and political activist. Graduate of Trinity College Dublin, he has written 4 acclaimed books & has 20 years experience in creativity education & cultural programme design.

Others: Irish Volunteers will be ready to assist. Feel free to ask questions!

Let’s get ready for the night! Don’t hesitate to register!

活動名稱︰愛爾蘭歷史酒吧文化夜

日期︰十月二十一日 (星期五)

時間︰晚上8 至 10時

地點︰The Cobblestones (77 King St N, Smithfield, Dublin, D07 TP22)

費用︰每位15歐。包小食。為鼓勵各人活學活用，收費不含飲品，同學需自行購買飲品。

活動詳情︰

- 學習愛爾蘭酒吧潛規則

- 了解愛爾蘭歷史及社會禁忌

- 了解愛爾蘭式幽默及吹水文化

- 親身體驗愛爾蘭人飲酒時進行的活動，例如：吟詩、唱飲酒歌、問答遊戲等等。

主持︰

Dave Lordan，著名愛爾蘭詩人、歷史學家、作家、教師及政治活躍份子。Dave 畢業於都柏林聖三一大學，他出版的4本書籍廣受好評，佢喺創意教育及文化項目設計方面擁有20年經驗。

其他：愛爾蘭義工亦會協助解答各類愛爾蘭文化生活問題。

呢個活動可以幫你投入了解愛爾蘭生活，唔好錯過，快啲報名啦！

We will have an online exhibition with The Museum of Childhood Ireland featuring children of Cantonese background growing up in Ireland. 

Date: Launch on 4th Nov. 

Place: On Childhood Museum of Ireland website:
https://museumofchildhood.ie/

Donation

Cantonese in Ireland is a registered non-profit organisation working to promote Cantonese culture in Ireland and support Cantonese migrants to integrate into Irish society. Since our start in 2022, all the work has been done by volunteers. However, we do have some regular expenses to keep the organisation running. Your support will make a big difference to us. As you can see above, we are still far from reaching out goal. Please help us so that we can continue this important work.
