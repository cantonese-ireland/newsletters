Dear Cantonese lovers,

各位親愛嘅粵語愛好者︰

September brings a new school year, a return to routine and, for many of us, a slightly busier pace. And this year, 25 September is Mid-Autumn Festival. 

With mooncakes, lanterns, moon-gazing and gatherings with family and friends, Mid-Autumn Festival is a celebration with a rich cultural tradition. 🥮🏮 For some, these are familiar traditions and childhood memories; for others, it may be a chance to discover something new about Cantonese culture - and perhaps try a mooncake or two along the way!

Whether you celebrate Mid-Autumn Festival every year or are discovering it for the first time, we hope you can enjoy a little of the festive atmosphere, share some food and time with family and friends, and have a lovely time together. 💛

From all of us at Cantonese in Ireland, 中秋節快樂！


九月返學、新學年開始，生活又慢慢忙返起上嚟；而今年嘅 9月25日，就係中秋節。

中秋節係一個好有特色嘅節日，有月餅、燈籠、賞月，亦有同屋企人朋友聚埋一齊嘅傳統。🥮🏮 對一啲人嚟講，呢啲係由細到大嘅節日回憶；對未慶祝過中秋節嘅朋友嚟講，亦可以係一個認識廣東話文化、試吓新嘢嘅好機會。

無論你係每年都會過中秋，定係今年第一次認識呢個節日，我哋都希望大家可以一齊感受吓中秋嘅氣氛，食吓月餅、賞吓月，同屋企人朋友享受一個開心嘅晚上。💛

Cantonese in Ireland 祝大家中秋節快樂！

Global Cantonese Writing Initiative for Youth

This year’s Global Cantonese Writing Initiative for Youth has now come to a close. We received 52 submissions, compared with 47 last year. Participants aged 5–18 took part from the UK, Hong Kong, China, USA, Ireland, Germany, Canada and Australia. 🌏

This year’s submissions:

Ages 5–9: 29

Ages 10–14: 15

Ages 15–18: 8

Total: 52 submissions

🇮🇪 We were also delighted to see four submissions from Ireland.

This year also saw the launch of the initiative’s official website, writingfromtheheart.org, bringing together the initiative details, guidelines and submission information, as well as selected works from last year. The website was generously sponsored by Harbour Kollective, including its design, development and hosting. Thank you for your support!

As part of the initiative, participants were also offered one-to-one online Cantonese writing sessions. A special thank you to Peg Chiu, author of Children Cantonese picture book 'The Tiger Under the Flyover', for giving her time to guide and encourage young writers, helping them develop their ideas, shape their stories and prepare their submissions. 🙏 Peg Chiu also wrote an article for Pulse HK News, encouraging readers to reconsider why we write in 書面語 (Standard Written Chinese) rather than Cantonese, and why we should make a greater effort to encourage our children to write in Cantonese instead.

    Thank you to all the young writers, parents, teachers, volunteers, sponsors and partners who supported the initiative.

    Through 「我手寫我心」— writing from the heart, we hope to encourage the next generation to express their creativity, thoughts and feelings in Cantonese. ✍️

    We look forward to seeing more young writers next year!

    Our Volunteers 我哋嘅義工

    In this issue, we would like to introduce you to our volunteer Albee!

    呢期通訊，我哋來介紹吓我哋嘅義工Albee啦！


    ----------------------------------


    Hi everyone, I’m Albee. I can’t believe I’ve already been in Ireland for a year! I’ve been studying here and experiencing life in Ireland. :)

    大家好，我係Albee。不知不覺已經嚟咗愛爾蘭一年啦，喺呢度讀書同體驗生活：）

    What brought you to Ireland? And what was your first impression of this place

    Two of my friends previously came to Ireland on working holiday and study programmes, and they both highly recommended Ireland. So I’ve always had a very positive impression of the country. I also wanted to continue my studies, and when I discovered that Irish universities offered courses related to my profession and interests, I decided to come here to study and experience life in Ireland.


    乜嘢令你嚟到愛爾蘭？⁠你對愛爾蘭嘅第一印象係點？

    以前曾經有兩位朋友嚟過愛爾蘭打工假期同留學，都一致推介愛爾蘭，所以我對愛爾蘭一直有深刻嘅印象。加上我想再進修，發現呢度嘅大學有同我職業同興趣相關嘅課程，所以就決定過嚟讀書生活。

    How did you start with Cantonese in Ireland? 

    I saw an event at my university co-organised by Cantonese in Ireland. As soon as I saw that it was about Cantonese, I signed up! That’s how I discovered the organisation. I was really happy to find activities in Ireland connected to Cantonese and Cantonese culture.


    你係點樣喺Cantonese in Ireland開始㗎？

    喺學校見到有Cantonese in Ireland合辦嘅活動，見到同廣東話有關嘅主題，就立即報咗名，然後就認識到呢個組織。好開心喺愛爾蘭搵到同廣東話文化有關嘅活動。

    What have you done with Cantonese in Ireland?

      I’ve taken part in Cantonese in Ireland’s mahjong competition and anniversary celebration. I really enjoyed the atmosphere and wanted to continue taking part in activities and meeting other people who share the same mother tongue. I later signed up as a volunteer, hoping to contribute in my own way to Cantonese culture in Ireland.

      你參與過 Cantonese in Ireland 嘅咩活動？

      我參加過Cantonese in Ireland嘅麻雀大賽同周年慶祝活動，覺得好有氣氛，想繼續參與有關嘅活動，同認識其他擁有共同母語嘅人。之後報名成為義工，希望為愛爾蘭嘅廣東話文化出一分力。

      What are your hopes for Cantonese immigrants in Ireland?

      I hope Cantonese-speaking immigrants in Ireland can settle into life here while also preserving and passing on Cantonese culture. I hope Cantonese in Ireland can be a place that brings everyone together. :)

      你對喺愛爾蘭嘅廣東話移民有咩期望？

      希望愛爾蘭嘅粵移民可以適應呢度嘅生活，同時可以保留粵文化。希望Cantonese in Ireland能夠成為一個凝聚到大家既地方：）

      Cantonese × Irish × AI: When Two Languages Meet Artificial Intelligence

      Have you ever thought about Cantonese and Irish being studied together in AI research?

      A recent study by Chit-Fung Lam and Elaine Uí Dhonnchadha brings the two languages together to examine their grammatical structures and explore the capabilities of large language models in Cantonese–Irish translation and linguistic analysis. The study also found that AI still has significant limitations in these areas, highlighting the continued importance of expert linguistic analysis and verification.

      The research forms part of the Parallel Grammar (ParGram) Project and is an interesting example of two very different languages being brought together through language technology research in Ireland.

      Paper: Grammar Engineering Meets LLMs: Development of Cantonese and Irish ParGram Treebanks
      Authors: Chit-Fung Lam and Elaine Uí Dhonnchadha
      Published: August 2026

      粵語 × Irish × AI：兩種語言遇上人工智能

      你有冇諗過，粵語同愛爾蘭語可以一齊做 AI 研究？

      最近由 Chit-Fung Lam 同 Elaine Uí Dhonnchadha 撰寫嘅研究，就將粵語同愛爾蘭語放埋一齊，研究兩種語言嘅語法，同時探討大型語言模型喺粵語同愛爾蘭語翻譯及語言分析方面嘅能力。研究結果亦指出，現時 AI 喺呢方面仍然有唔少限制，語言專家嘅分析同核實仍然非常重要。

      呢項研究屬於 Parallel Grammar (ParGram) Project，亦係一個幾有趣嘅例子，展示兩種背景同結構都好唔同嘅語言，可以喺愛爾蘭一齊出現喺語言科技研究入面。

      研究： Grammar Engineering Meets LLMs: Development of Cantonese and Irish ParGram Treebanks
      作者： Chit-Fung Lam、Elaine Uí Dhonnchadha
      發表： 2026年8月

      Congratulations, Jessica — Proudly Hong Kong, Proudly Irish!

      A big congratulations to Jessica Hunter, this year’s Kildare Rose, as she returns to the Rose of Tralee journey 10 years after first taking part! 🌹 For anyone new to Ireland, the Rose of Tralee is a much-loved Irish festival celebrating women from Ireland and Irish communities around the world, and their stories and connections to Ireland. Jessica grew up between Kildare and Hong Kong, with an Irish mam and a Hong Kong dad, and has spoken proudly about embracing “the blend of both cultures” that have shaped her. As a Cantonese community in Ireland, it’s especially lovely to see someone with our heritage represented on such a recognisably Irish stage. Congratulations, Jessica! We’re delighted to celebrate your journey and wish you every success!

      恭喜 Jessica — 以香港愛爾蘭為傲！🌹

      事隔 10 年再次踏上 Rose of Tralee 嘅旅程，恭喜 Jessica Hunter 成為今年嘅 Kildare Rose🌹 Rose of Tralee係愛爾蘭一個歷史悠久、深受大家喜愛嘅節慶。來自愛爾蘭各地，以及世界各地愛爾蘭社群嘅參選者透過喺選舉，展現佢哋嘅才華，分享佢哋嘅同愛爾蘭嘅連繫嘅故事。

      Jessica 喺 Kildare 同香港兩地成長，媽媽係愛爾蘭人，爸爸係香港人，佢話佢為自己嘅雙文化背景感到自豪。對我哋喺愛爾蘭嘅粵社群嚟講，見到一位有香港背景嘅女性喺愛爾蘭呢個咁具代表性嘅舞台上發光，實在令人感到特別親切。
