各位親愛嘅粵語愛好者︰

lung4 maa5 zing1 san4 (龍馬精神) means the spirit of strength and courage. It encourages people to face challenges bravely, never give up, and keep moving forward with determination.

Lunar New Year is the most significant festival for Cantonese people worldwide. To celebrate, we have organised a series of activities here in Ireland! 

恭喜發財 ! 祝大家喺愛爾蘭過得開心順利, 新春大吉，龍馬精神常伴左右！今個農曆年，我哋精心籌備咗一系列慶祝活動，希望大家雖然身處愛爾蘭，都可以感受到濃濃嘅過年味，一齊開心慶祝！

Dublin Vikings Dragon Boat are hosting an open day tryout on 21 February – a perfect chance for anyone with no dragon boat experience to give it a go. Dragon boat racing dates back over 2,000 years and is closely linked to the Tuen Ng (Dragon Boat) Festival, where racing and eating jung (glutinous rice dumplings) honour remembrance, community spirit, and Cantonese cultural heritage. The Cantonese community in Ireland also has an active team that races every June, and the Dublin Vikings have supported us tirelessly with training year after year – we couldn’t ask for a better partner. If you’re interested, please contact the Dublin Vikings (details on the poster).

Dublin Vikings Dragon Boat 將會喺 2 月 21 號 舉辦開放日暨試扒活動，無論你之前有冇扒過龍舟，都係一個好好嘅機會喺愛爾蘭親身試下！
龍舟比賽已經有超過二千年歷史，同端午節有密切嘅關係。喺端午節，我哋透過扒龍舟同包糉食糉嚟紀念先人、凝聚社群，同時傳承廣東文化。
愛爾蘭嘅粵語社群呢幾年嚟都有一隊活躍嘅龍舟隊，每年六月都會參與喺Carlow舉行嘅本地賽事。而Dublin Vikings呢幾年來一直都好支持我哋，年年都提供場地、器材，甚至幫手訓練我哋，真係再好不過嘅合作夥伴。
如果你有興趣參加，請直接聯絡 Dublin Vikings（聯絡資料請參考海報）。
Our Volunteers 我哋嘅義工

In this issue, we would like to introduce you our volunteer - Ian. 

喺今期嘅通訊，我哋想同大家介紹吓我哋嘅義工 Ian. 

Tell us something about yourself.  

Hi, my name is Ian Hau, British born Chinese. Grew up in London and have been in Dublin just over a year now.

Why do you volunteer with Cantonese in ireland ?

I found out about Cantonese in Ireland via an event last year that was advertised on eventbrite. After attending, I decided that I would like to be involved to meet people and build my network here. Since I'm new to the country / city of Dublin. And to maintain in touch with my cantonese roots since I don't have any family here.


What do you want to achieve volunteering with Cantonese in Ireland?
I hope to build my network, meet new people, be involved with events / volunteering activities. Also network for jobs and career opportunities. And participate in the group for personal development reasons. 

不如你介紹吓自己。

Hi! 我叫侯俊賢，係一個英國出生嘅華人。我係倫敦長大，而家喺都柏林住咗一年多。

點解你會喺我哋<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>呢個機構做義工嘅？

我係去年喺一個活動中透過Eventbrite發現咗愛爾蘭有關粵語嘅活動。參加完之後，我決定希望加入，認識新朋友，並喺呢度擴展自己嘅人脈。因為我係新嚟呢個國家／城市，都柏林，而我喺呢度冇家人，所以我想保持同自己嘅粵語根源聯繫。


你喺<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>做義工嘅目標係咩？

我希望可以建立人脈，認識新朋友，參與活動／義工工作，並且為搵工作同職業機會建立聯繫。另外，我亦想參加呢個團體，為咗自己嘅個人發展。
