Recruiting of volunteers for Bloomsday☘️

Hey… are there any Cantonese speakers who love Anglo-Irish literature in Ireland?

We’re looking for volunteers to help with our Bloomsday event, celebrating Joyce’s masterpieces in a multicultural way! If you are interested in James Joyce, or simply want to share your enthusiasm, we’d love to have you on board. You can be part of the Bloomsday Walking Tour and Movie screening on the 14th of June, and follow Bloom's steps in Ulysses!

Interested? Email us at hello@cantoneseireland.ie! Let’s bring Joyce to life in Cantonese on the special day!

黃導演特別提到愛爾蘭“好有性格”，俾到佢一種往日同自然親近嘅感覺，尤其係真火嘅暖爐🔥同埋無處不在嘅真花真草🌹。

Volunteer Interview 義工訪談

Angela

Hi I’m Angela currently based in cork.

你好，我係Angela，而家住喺Cork。

To be honest it was my degree which brings to me cork, I was debating between Ireland and the UK, but due to various circumstances I chose Ireland and am currently studying my dream course in UCC. I’m’ loving it so far!

其因為我嘅學位課程，所以我嚟咗Cork。記得當時我考慮緊去英國定愛爾蘭讀書，最後因為種種原因選擇咗愛爾蘭，而家喺UCC讀緊我夢寐以求嘅課程。直到而家，我仲係好鍾意呢度！

I started with CII three years ago when I first arrived cork. I was scrolling through Facebook and wonder if there’s any Cantonese community in Ireland therefore I reached out to Peg and seemingly it’s one of the best choice I’ve made so far.

三年前我啱啱嚟到Cork，亦嗰陣開始接觸CII。當時想睇下喺愛爾蘭有冇粵語社群，終於喺Facebook度無意間發現咗CII。我跟Peg聯絡，結果發現呢個決定係我至今做過最好嘅選擇之一。

I’m sort of helping with scattered tiny little bits among the community. I was one of the guest speaker of Alice’s podcast probably one or two years ago and assisted in photo exhibition, then moved on to post making and volunteered in a few sessions in the storytelling in cork library. Recently helped with writing an email to a new library for their interest in setting up our photo exhibition.

我主要係幫手處理社群入面零散嘅細節工作。我大概一兩年前曾經做過Alice嘅Podcast嘉賓，亦有幫手影相展覽，之後開始設計宣傳貼文，亦有喺Cork圖書館嘅故事分享活動度做義工。最近仲幫手寫咗封Email俾一間新圖書館，希望佢哋對我哋嘅攝影展有興趣。

My hope for Cantonese immigrants is to be proud of your culture and your identity! Ireland is a beautiful country with so many lovely people coming from a diverse background and they always love to hear about our culture ! (Speaking of experiences). There are indeed quite a bit of overlapping with the Cantonese and Irish culture so it’s always amazing to hear about them.

我希望粵移民可以為自己嘅文化同身份感到自豪。愛爾蘭係一個好靚嘅國家，入面有好多來自唔同文化背景嘅善良人，根據我個人嘅親身經驗，佢哋都對我哋嘅文化充滿興趣！其實粵文化同愛爾蘭文化有唔少相似之處，每次交流都覺得好神奇！

It could be really challenging to adapt into a whole new different place so reach out to the community whenever necessary. They are all nice people 😉

適應一個完全新嘅地方可能會好有挑戰性。如果有需要，記住隨時聯絡社群！大家都係好好人㗎！😉

For those who’re interested in studying at UCC, feel free to contact me for a campus tour if you wish!

如果有興趣喺UCC讀書，歡迎隨時搵我，我可以帶你參觀校園！

Best regards,

Angela

Online Cantonese Stories on Saturdays 週六網上廣東話故事時間

Led by our experienced pre-school teacher, Kiki, and storytelling offered by teachers, students, parents, native Cantonese speakers or second generation Cantonese immigrants, our FREE online Cantonese Storytelling on every 1st Saturday of each month, from 11-11.40am. 

由我哋經驗豐富嘅幼稚園老師 Kiki 帶領，故事團隊由一班老師、學生、家長、粵母語人士或第二代粵移民組成。我哋每個月嘅第一個星期六，朝早 11 點到11 點 40 分，免費為你哋喺網上講粵語故事。
