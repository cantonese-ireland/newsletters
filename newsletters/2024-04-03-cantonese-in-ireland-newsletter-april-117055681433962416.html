Reports 活動回顧

St. Patrick’s Day Parade at Dun Laoghaire and Cork

St. Patrick’s Day 遊行

Wow! At least 70 to 90 thousands people were watching Cantonese in Ireland holding up the beautiful flowerboards parading together in St Patrick's Day Parade in Dun Laoghaire and Cork City. Some people in the audience shouted with us in Cantonese: "I love you."

Hey, don't let it stop. Let's do it again next year.

嘩！CORK同DUN LAOGHAIRE遊行都有我哋<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>。家吓最少有七萬至九萬人望住我哋舉住嗰啲靚靚花牌一齊行。仲有觀眾同我哋一齊用廣東話大叫︰「我愛你」。

嗱，唔好俾佢停，下年再玩過。

Online Photo Exhibition-
Cantonese Floweboards: From Hong Kong to Ireland

<<廣東花牌︰由香港到愛爾蘭>>網上攝影展

We are so delighted to work with the Museum of Childhood Ireland again this year. After the online photo exhibition 'We are Cantonese Irish' last year, this year, we have another online photo exhibition 'Cantonese Floweboards: From Hong Kong to Ireland' being hosted by them. Free online Political Participation Workshop (in Cantonese)

免費網上工作坊︰由投開始愛生活 (粵語)

The district council electoral is almost here! You will have the opportunity to vote this June. 

You only need to be a resident (RESIDENT, non-citizen CITIZEN) living in Ireland to have the opportunity to vote for your regional representative.

Ireland's electoral system is a bit complicated, but we are happy to have our 'Cantonese in Ireland' Vice President Alice explain it to us in Cantonese in this online seminar, and everyone can register as voters together in real time.

Register now for this important event of ours and learn about the Irish electoral system, exercise your rights and fulfil your responsibilities.

地區選舉就到喇！今年六月你就有機會投神聖一票。

你只需要係一個喺愛爾蘭居住嘅居民 (RESIDENT, 非公民 CITIZEN)，都有機會投票選你嘅地區代表㗎。

愛爾蘭選舉制度有啲複雜，我哋好開心有<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>副會長Alice為我哋用粵語喺呢個網上研討會講解一吓，大家仲可以即時一齊登記做選民。

快啲登記我哋呢個好重要嘅活動，認識愛爾蘭選舉制度，行使你嘅權利，盡你嘅責任。

Cantonese Picture Book 'Tiger Under the Flyover' Group Purchase

粵文兒童繪本<<橋底老虎>>團購

The first children's picture book in Cantonese to introduce Hong Kong's intangible cultural heritage "Beating the Villain"

This book has Cantonese, Mandarin, Cantonese Jyutping and an audio file. It is very suitable for overseas parents to help their children learn Cantonese, Cantonese pronunciation and Traditional Chinese characters.

Anyone interested in purchasing can contact us to arrange a group purchase (price: €12 approximately, may include free shipping)


首本以粵文介紹香港非物質文化遺產「打小人」嘅兒童繪本。

本書有齊粵文、華文、粵拼同語音檔。非常適合海外家長，幫助小朋友學習粵語、粵拼及漢字認讀。

    有興趣購買嘅朋友可以聯絡我哋安排團購  (書價約︰12 歐，或可免運費)
