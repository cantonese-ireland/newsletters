Coffee & Movie Meetup: Obedience

📽 About the Film:
Directed by Wong Siu Pong, Obedience is an observational documentary capturing the street life of Hung Hom, Hong Kong. Filmed over three years and edited over another three, the film offers an unfiltered glimpse into working-class life. Rather than following a conventional narrative, it relies on raw visuals to tell its story, inviting audiences to reflect on often-overlooked corners of the city. This film is one of the selected films in Dublin International Film Festival.

Movie & Lunch Meetup: The Way We Talk

📽 About the Film:
The Way We Talk is a heartfelt documentary that explores the lives of people with speech impairments. Through the stories of individuals from diverse backgrounds, the film delves into their struggles and triumphs in finding ways to communicate.

Lead actress Chung Suet Ying won the Golden Horse Award for Best Actress for her powerful performance, deeply moving audiences with her portrayal of overcoming personal barriers. Beyond speech therapy, the documentary highlights resilience, family support, and societal understanding.

This film is one of the selected films in Dublin International Film Festival.

電影午餐聚：《看我今天怎麼說》

📽 電影簡介：

《看我今天怎麼說》係一部動人嘅紀錄片，深入探討言語障礙患者嘅世界。透過幾位唔同背景嘅主角，電影講述佢哋點樣克服語言上嘅障礙，努力尋找同世界溝通嘅方法。

主演鍾雪瑩更憑此片榮獲金馬獎最佳女主角，佢演技精湛，將角色嘅內心掙扎同個人成長細膩咁呈現喺銀幕上。呢部作品唔單止係關於言語治療，更探討個人意志、家庭支持同埋社會理解嘅重要，係一個極具啟發性嘅故事。

《看我今天怎麼說》乃都柏林國際電影節選播作品之一。

This year's Awakening of Insects is fast approaching! 🐛⚡

Every year during the Awakening of Insects, the area under Goose Neck Bridge in Hong Kong is filled with smoke and fire 🔥 as local spirit mediums become incredibly busy helping people perform the ritual of "beating petty people" (da siu yan). The origin of this tradition actually comes from driving away pests 🦟, and Goose Neck Bridge itself has transformed over time from a small wooden bridge into a modern four-lane overpass 🚗🚕.

📖 This Saturday, Peg Chiu, author of The Tiger Under the Flyover, will share the history and transformation of Goose Neck Bridge through her storybook. While the environment may change, people’s hopes for a safe and prosperous life remain the same.

Peg will also invite children to take turns reading the story using Jyutping (the romanisation of Cantonese) , and we will also learn how to write ✍️ a few Traditional Han characters.

Date: 1st March 2025 (Saturday)
Time: 11-1140am (GMT)
Location: ONLINE

“Papa” is here!

The Hong Kong film 'Papa' starring Sean Lau Ching-wan opens in Dublin TOMORROW! “Papa” tells the heart-wrenching story of Yuen Wing-nin, a tea restaurant owner whose life is shattered when his eldest son tragically takes the lives of his wife and daughter. The film explores Yuen’s struggle with survivor’s guilt and his efforts to maintain his role as a father while grappling with the complex relationship with his son.Don’t miss this emotional journey on the big screen. Volunteer Interview 義工訪問
Ethan

Hi! My name is Ethan. I have moved to Ireland since 2022, and I am currently still a student studying for the Leaving Cert. 

Hi! 我叫 Ethan, 自從2022年搬咗去愛爾蘭，而家仲係學生，準備考Leaving Cert。

In TY, my school made it mandatory for everyone to volunteer in a charity for a week. However, I decided to stay long-term because I have always been heavily interested in Cantonese culture (especially the food!). Not only will I get to introduce others to it, but I will also continue learning about it myself.

喺TY (Transition Year) 嗰年，我學校規定每個人都要做一個星期義工。不過我就決定長期做落去，因為我一直都對廣東文化有好大興趣，特別係食嘢！唔單止可以介紹畀人，自己都可以繼續學多啲。

I believe that although it is important to embrace a new life and a new culture moving to Ireland, it is also just as important to remember where you came from, what your roots are. Cantonese culture is very vibrant, dynamic and colorful, and I hope it stays alive within me for years to come. Also, I believe everyone needs to try 叉燒包 at least once in a lifetime :) 

我覺得，搬去愛爾蘭接受新生活同文化係好重要，但同時都唔可以唔記得自己嘅根，喺邊度嚟。廣東文化好有活力同色彩，我希望佢喺我身上可以長存好多年。仲有，我覺得每個人一生都應該試一次叉燒包！:)
