Christmas 2022 聖誕

Dear Cantonese lovers, 

各位親愛嘅粵語愛好者︰

(For English, please scroll down)

感謝各位喺今年對我哋嘅支持，借呢個機會，我哋想同你哋分享吓 <<粵語。愛爾蘭>> 今年嘅工作成果。


里程碑：

  • 本組織於本年1月成立

  • 並於5月跟政府成式註冊 (註冊號碼: 719937)


今年達成咗嘅任務:

  1. 總共舉辦咗46項活動，包括︰農曆新年、端午節、中秋節、各項展覽、高基市圖書館定期實體雙語講故事時間，定期網上粵語講故事、以及多次粵語社交聚會。
  2. 以上活動總共有600參與人次，當中300人次係非粵語母語人士。

  3. 跟唔同機構合作，包括︰政府機構 ( 圖書館、古蹟局、都柏林市議會 ) ，其他非牟利組織 ( 譬如社區中心、愛爾蘭兒童博物館、愛爾蘭龍舟會、多元文化大使，冚唪唥粵文讀本 ) 及私人公司 (譬如好世界、亞洲行、港式麵包蛋糕西餅 ) 。

  4. 有幸喺183個機構之中獲選為45間其中之一，參與由愛爾蘭社福企業 (SEI) 舉辦嘅管理訓練課程，被視為「有潛力解決愛爾蘭境內非常迫切嘅社會問題」其中一間機構。

  5. 獲得幾項愛爾蘭政府或非牟利組織提供嘅金錢資助，當中包括︰愛爾蘭政府兒童青年平等機會及傷健局嘅融合基金、FORSA小額資助、SEI助學金、以及都柏林巴士社會精神資助金。

  6. 組織不斷壯大：由起初只有現時嘅7名委員會會員「 一腳踢」，我哋係 短短數月，義工人數已經增至26名，並分別隸屬於9個唔同嘅小組委員會。

  7. 每月通訊名單喺數個月內由零上升至258位。

  8. 多個社交平臺得到1471名追蹤者，只係睇最近3個月內嘅數據，我哋平台訊息已觸及4萬人次。


面對嘅挑戰：

  1. 日常開支嘅資金來源：以上列出嘅所有資助，有條款規定必須使用於指定活動，因此我哋需要其他資金來源以補助我哋嘅行政費用，包括會計費用、繪圖軟件、Zoom年費等等。

  2. 全新正式官方網站：呢個係我哋希望新一年能夠處理嘅最重要項目之一。我哋而家用緊嘅網頁，只能夠暫時擺放一版，全部連結到不同地方，非常唔專業。我哋急切需要一個正式嘅網站，先至可以令到我哋嘅組織繼續發展。即使我哋嘅義工願意自己去建立呢個網站，仍然需要150歐羅嘅網頁托管費用。

我哋其實好唔願意要咁樣開聲，但係即使只係小小嘅5歐羅，都會幫到我哋好多。我哋希望你能夠捐款資助我哋，以助我哋嘅工作能夠繼續進行。咁樣，我哋就可以繼續喺愛爾蘭推動粵語及粵文化，令愛爾蘭更能聽到我哋呢班粵語愛爾蘭人嘅聲音。

Thank you for your support this year. We think this is a good opportunity to make a bit of a Wrap-Up so that we can share our achievements this year. 

Milestones:

  • Organisation set up in January
  • Registered with the government in May (register number: 719937)

Our achievements this year:

  1. We have organised 46 events, including events to celebrate the Lunar New Year, Dragon Boat Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival, exhibitions, regular Cantonese storytelling online and in Cork city library, regular Cantonese social meetups

  2. From all the amazing events above, we have served 600 headcounts, of which 300 of them non-native Cantonese users.

  3. We have worked with different government departments (libraries, Heritage Council, Dublin City Council) and other not-for-profit organisations (such as community centres, Museum of Childhood Ireland, Irish Dragon Boat Association, Intercultural Ambassadors, Hambaanglaang Publisher in Hong Kong) or private companies (such as Good World, Asia Market, the Hong Kong Bakery, etc)

  4. We got selected by SEI Social Entrepreneurs Ireland to attend their Idea Academy, as one of the 45 out of 183 organisations that applied, and be identified as "having high potential to have a significant impact on pressing social issues that are facing Irish society"

  5. We got funding from the Department of Children, Equality, Disability, Integration and Youth, the Forsa Small Grant, SEI Bursary Payment, and the Dublin Bus Community Spirit Grant

  6. Our team is also growing strong. We started with 7 committee members, and we now have grown to 26 volunteers, belonging to 9 different sub-committees.

  7. Our mailing list has grown from 0 to 258 in a few months. 

  8. Our multiple Social Media platform has gathered 1471 followers, with post-reach of approximately 40,000 in the last 3 months alone.

Challenges ahead: 

  1. Funding for regular expenses: One of the challenges we are facing is that - all money from various fundings has to be used on specific events. As a result, we are required to raise funds to cover our administration costs. e.g. accountant, Graphic design software, Zoom membership, etc...
  2. We need a proper website! One of the big projects we hope to handle in the New Year. The current website is a one-page affair that we put up temporarily, but we really really need a proper website as the organisation grows, and that will cost about €150 for the hosting even if the volunteers make it themselves. 

We hate to ask, but please, if you can spare even €5, it's going to help a lot. If you want us to continue our work, to promote and support Cantonese language and culture in Ireland, please donate below.
