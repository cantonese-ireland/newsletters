Dear Cantonese lovers,

各位親愛嘅粵語愛好者︰

April was a relatively quiet month. Aside from our regular bilingual storytelling sessions held online and at Cork City Library on the first and second Saturdays of the month, we also hosted an online Cantonese writing workshop. Although there weren’t many events, the committee members and volunteers of Cantonese in Ireland have been working hard behind the scenes to prepare for upcoming activities in May and June.

Cantonese in Ireland is a fully volunteer-run organisation, and we would like to take this opportunity to sincerely thank every volunteer who has devoted their time and effort.


四月喺一個比較平靜嘅月份。除咗逢第一、二個星期六喺網上及高基市圖書館嘅雙語故事活動之外，我哋亦舉辦咗一個網上粵文工作坊。雖然活動唔多，但粵語愛爾蘭一班幹事義工仍然默默努力，積極籌備嚟緊五月同六月嘅活動。粵語愛爾蘭係一個全由義工運作嘅機構，所以，喺度，我哋衷心多謝每一位付出心力同時間嘅義工。

Volunteer Interview 義工訪談

Max Yu

Do you know that Max is our fresh new chairperson of committee of Cantonese in Ireland this year? 

你知唔知道，Max 係今年新當選嘅「粵語愛爾蘭」委員會主席？

Let's hear what he has to share with us:

一齊嚟聽吓佢嘅分享：

Hi, I'm Max. I've been living in Ireland for two and a half years. I was born in Hong Kong and later moved to Singapore and Australia. I'm a behavioural scientist and an Assistant Professor. I relocated to Ireland after accepting an offer from Maynooth University. Brexit made Ireland an even more attractive destination, and so far, I believe I’ve made the right choice. 

大家好，我係 Max，我喺愛爾蘭住咗兩年半。我喺香港出世，之後移居到新加坡及澳洲。我係一位行為科學家，亦係一位大學副教授。我因為接受咗 Maynooth University 嘅聘請而搬嚟愛爾蘭。英國脫歐令愛爾蘭變得更加吸引，而到目前為止，我都覺得自己選擇移居愛爾蘭係一個正確嘅決定。

I came across the Cantonese in Ireland website while searching for Hong Kong or Cantonese-based social groups for my wife. We attended a gathering at CHQ and made some friends. One thing led to another, and I joined the committee a few months later. 

剛到愛爾蘭，人生路不熟，為咗幫我太太搵香港人或者講粵語嘅社群，我發現「粵語愛爾蘭」嘅網站。我哋去咗一次喺 CHQ 舉辦嘅聚會，識咗啲新朋友。之後因緣際會，我幾個月後就加入咗委員會。

Since joining the committee, I’ve been working with others to develop a practical long-term strategy that aligns with our mission and values. This includes improving brand clarity and creating a sustainability plan. As the Communications Officer for the past two years, I was the main point of contact with the public, and I also helped secure funding and organize events. 

自從加入以嚟，我一直同其他委員合作，制定一個切實可行、跟我哋使命同價值觀一致嘅長遠發展策略，包括︰提升品牌定位同建立可持續發展計劃。過去兩年，我擔任傳訊主任，係幫外對外聯絡以及協助申請資金同籌辦活動。

Now, as the new Chairperson, I hope Cantonese in Ireland can continue to support Cantonese immigrants, preserve the language, and share our culture. My wife and I have benefitted greatly from the community in many ways, and we hope to give back.

作為新任主席，我希望「粵語愛爾蘭」可以繼續支持粵移民群體，傳承我哋嘅語言，並推廣我哋嘅文化。我太太同我喺呢個社群中獲益良多，我哋都好希望可以回饋社區。
Report 報告

On 13th April, the sun was shining brightly. Yet, a small group of children gathered for Canto+Eng storytime in Cork City Library.

One little child spoke fluent Cantonese — it was clear parents had put a lot of love and effort to help keep the mother tongue. It was the first time for another child to learn Cantonese, the child beamed with pride reading out the new Cantonese words. The young helper sat listening with a beautiful smile to the story about the Peak Tram in Hong Kong and told mum how enjoyable the story was afterwards.

After story time, the children used their fingers to draw a tree, tapping and sketching as they listened to a few adults admiring their artwork — all in Cantonese.

An hour drifted by, calm and full of joy.


四月十三號，陽光普照，高基市圖書館內幾個小朋友人少少聽故仔。一位小小朋友一口流利粵語，爹哋媽咪真係俾咗好多心機幫小朋友KEEP住母語。又有小朋友初學粵語，每讀到一個粵語詞彙都一面自豪。小助教笑咪咪聽住一個關香港山頂纜車小高嘅故事，事後同媽咪話好鍾意個故仔。故事之後，小朋友用手指點點點畫樹，一邊畫一邊聽住幾個大人用粵語讚嘆佢哋嘅大作。輕輕鬆鬆一個鐘頭悠然而過。


Angela and her team represented Cantonese in Ireland at a consultation held in University College Cork on 10th April. The consultation was part of the development of the UCC Race Equality Action Plan 2025.

At the session, we raised the following points:

  1. We hope there will be greater awareness of the diversity within ethnic Chinese and Asian communities. Cantonese is a distinct language within the broader Sinitic language family. Our heritage and culture differ from those associated with Mandarin, and as such, our community has unique needs.

  2. We advocate for the use of Cantonese and Traditional Chinese characters when different languages are included in official materials, to better reflect linguistic diversity.

Angela 同佢嘅團隊代表「粵語愛爾蘭」，喺4月10日參加咗高基大學舉辦嘅《2025年種族平等行動計劃》諮詢會。

喺會議入面，我哋提出咗以下幾點意見：

  1. 我哋希望大眾可以更加認識華人／亞洲族群之間嘅多樣性。粵語係漢語語系入面其中一種語言，我哋嘅語言文化同華語雖相近但亦有很多分別，因此之故，我哋社群嘅需要亦跟華語社群唔一樣。

  2. 如果官方材料當中會包含多種語言，我哋希望材料會有粵文及繁體字嘅版本，以更準確反映愛爾蘭社群嘅多樣性。
