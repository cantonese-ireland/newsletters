Cantonese in Ireland 2024 Event Review

Cantonese in Ireland celebrated an extraordinary year of cultural promotion, community engagement, and advocacy, organising 50 diverse events that reached over 90,000 people, including native Cantonese speakers, heritage learners, and non-native participants. Here’s an overview of some of the year’s highlights:

Cultural Highlights

  • Dublin Lunar New Year: The Cantonese community showcased their heritage with traditional games like jungle chess and mahjong, along with online storytelling sessions sharing Cantonese traditions.
  • St. Patrick’s Day Parades: Cantonese groups brought multicultural vibrancy to parades in Cork and Dun Laoghaire.
  • Dragon Boat Festival: A thrilling event with races, media coverage, and awards presented by the Vice Chair.
  • Anniversary Celebration: Recognized achievements and honored dedicated volunteers.
  • Cinema Promotion: Efforts to bring Cantonese films to Irish cinemas.
  • Workshops and Exhibitions: Held in libraries and a college, these events celebrated Cantonese culture, fostering cross-cultural understanding and appreciation.
  • Storytelling Sessions: Held online and at Cork City Library, these sessions captivated audiences with Cantonese folklore.

Promoting Engagement and Integration

  • Immigration Focus Group: Advocated for inclusive policies for immigrant communities.
  • Civic Engagement: Active in Cork and Dublin PPNs to influence local initiatives.
  • Job Market Workshop: Offered practical support for immigrants on CVs and networking.

Advocacy and Recognition

  • RTÉ Interview: Vice Chair spotlighted Cantonese contributions at Dublin History Festival.
  • Brief introduction of Cantonese language and tradition at award ceremony at Dragonboat Festival
  • Cantonese member Irene interviewed with Cork radio 96FM

    粵語愛爾蘭2024年活動回顧

    粵語愛爾蘭喺推廣文化、促進社區參與同倡導方面，喺2024年交出咗一份亮麗嘅成績表。我哋全年舉辦咗50場多元化活動，令超過90,000人睇見我哋、聽到我哋嘅聲音。以下係今年部分精彩回顧：

    主要活動

    傳揚粵語粵文化

    • 都柏林農曆新年：我哋喺慶祝活動中展示咗傳統文化，包括玩鬥獸棋同麻雀，仲舉辦咗網上故事分享會，傳揚廣東文化同傳統。
    • 聖博德節巡遊：我哋參加咗高基同Dun Laoghaire嘅巡遊活動，為活動增添多元文化嘅色彩。
    • 端午節龍舟競渡：一年一度嘅重點活動，有刺激嘅龍舟比賽，仲獲得咗主流媒體報導，得獎隊伍由副主席頒發獎品。
    • 週年慶祝活動：表揚我哋對社群嘅貢獻，並向四位傑出嘅義工頒發獎項，感謝佢哋嘅服務。
    • 電影推廣：致力將香港電影帶入愛爾蘭戲院。
    • 工作坊同展覽：喺圖書館同一所學院舉行活動，展示粵語文化，促進文化理解同欣賞。
    • 故事分享會：喺網上同高基市圖書館舉行，以粵語分享故事，吸引大人同細路，推動文化認識。

    推動參與同融合

    • 移民政策焦點小組：參與政府領導嘅小組，倡導更加包容嘅移民政策。
    • 公民參與：積極參加高基同都柏林嘅公民參與網絡（PPN），影響地方項目同政策。
    • 就業工作坊：為移民提供實用嘅建議，例如改善履歷同建立人脈網絡。

    倡導同表揚

    • RTÉ訪問：副主席喺都柏林歷史節期間接受RTÉ訪問，展示香港人對愛爾蘭文化嘅貢獻。
    • 籍喺龍舟競渡頒獎，簡單介紹粵語粵傳統，令現場觀眾更加了解粵文化特色。
    • 高基粵社群成員Irene接受高基96FM電台訪問，分享粵社群喺愛爾蘭嘅生活點滴。

    Dora

    Hi, I’m Dora. I’m 17 and turning 18 this December. I’m currently studying undergraduate medicine at UCD, having completed my Leaving Cert last year. Born and raised in Hong Kong until the age of 10, my parents decided to send my brother and me to Ireland for further education, believing we would have a happier life here. Looking back, I’m incredibly grateful for their decision—pursuing medicine wouldn’t have been possible if I had stayed in Hong Kong. After almost seven years in Ireland, I’ve grown to feel a strong sense of belonging here.

    你好，我叫Dora，今年17歲，12月就會滿18歲。我而家喺UCD讀醫科，啱啱喺去年完成咗Leaving Cert。我喺香港出世同長大，直至10歲嗰年，父母決定送我同哥哥去愛爾蘭讀書，因為佢哋相信我哋喺愛爾蘭讀書會更加開心。睇返轉頭，我好感激佢哋嘅決定。我如果仲喺香港，我應該無可能讀到醫科。喺愛爾蘭生活咗差唔多七年，我開始對呢個地方有歸屬感。

    In 2021, I discovered Cantonese in Ireland (CII) through posts my mom shared with me. I had no idea an organization for Cantonese speakers existed in Ireland. At the time, I was in 5th year and had more free time, so I signed up to volunteer, eager to connect with other Cantonese speakers and contribute to this meaningful community. Volunteering with CII has been a new and rewarding experience. I’ve worked on social media posts, assisted with the monthly newsletter (which was particularly challenging!), and helped with captions for the photo exhibition—a unique and enjoyable task.

    2021年，媽媽傳粵語愛爾蘭啲 POST畀我睇，就係咁，我發現咗粵語愛爾蘭。我嗰時先發現，原來愛爾蘭有一個專為粵人士設立嘅組織。嗰陣我喺中五，有多啲時間，所以報咗名做義工，想認識愛爾蘭嘅粵群體，亦想為呢個有意義嘅機構出一分力。義工工作對我嚟講係一個全新嘅體驗，亦收穫良多。我幫手做過社交媒體post，亦有參與月訊嘅制作（呢個任務特別有挑戰性！），仲幫過攝影展覽寫相片說明，呢件工作對我嚟講幾特別，又有趣。

    Although I took a break during my 6th year to focus on the Leaving Cert, I’ve continued volunteering in university and plan to stay involved. Through CII, I’ve gained valuable skills, met people from diverse backgrounds, and deepened my appreciation for the work they do. I look forward to contributing to more Cantonese projects in the future!

    喺中六準備Leaving Cert嗰陣，我暫停咗一段時間，但而家讀大學，我又可以重新投入CII嘅義務工作。打算未來都會繼續參與。喺CII，我學識咗同唔同背景嘅人合作，亦喺佢哋身上學到好多嘢。我好期待將來可以參與更多唔同嘅項目，推廣粵語粵文化！
