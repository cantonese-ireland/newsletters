Free Irish lesson 免費愛爾蘭語課程

Living in Ireland yet having zero clue about the local language? Can't read the Irish road signs? Can't pronounce Siobhan, Micheál, Tadhg or Gráinne? Know little about the Irish culture? Do not get what the craic is when people are laughing? Cannot help with your own child's Irish homework?

Now here is the chance!

We are honoured to have Dr. Qiu from University College Dublin (UCD) to design and deliver this Beginner Irish Language Course for all who speak Cantonese or English or both.

The cost? FREE, FREE, FREE! (Speak thrice for important matters).

And you can take away CEFR A1 level in Irish when the course ends.

What can I get after I join the class?

1. A bilingual or even trilingual brain

2. A mouth that can start convos with locals in Irish

3. A chance to get out of your safety zone, broaden your knowledge

4. Acquaint more friends from the class

我哋好榮幸邀請到喺都柏林大學（UCD）任教嘅邱方哲教授親自設計及教授初階愛爾蘭語文課程。

而呢個課程學費全免。係呀！免費！免費！免費！（重要嘅事情要講三次）

學完之後，你可以帶走類近歐洲共同語言參考標準A1等級嘅愛爾蘭語。

參加我哋嘅課程，你可以得到：

1. 一個雙語或三語嘅靈活腦袋；

2. 一個可以同本地人開啓聊天嘅嘴巴；

3. 一個開闊眼界，解鎖更多知識面嘅機會；

4. 認識更多喺係愛爾蘭講廣東話嘅朋友。
