Bilingual Storytelling in Cantonese and English at Cork City Library

We will be having a bilingual storytelling in Cantonese & English at Cork City Library this coming Saturday (8 Oct 2022) from 11am to 11.40am, and also every 2nd Saturday at the same time.  

我哋今個星期六 (十月八號)，會喺高基市圖書館有一個廣東話英語雙語故事時間，由朝早十一點到十一點四十分。我哋以後每個月第二個星期六同樣時間都會喺高基市圖書館同大家見面。

We need your help to continue these programmes

If you have enjoyed these free events and would love to see us continue our work, please donate if you can. Even €5 is helpful. And if you can sign up to monthly contribution it's even better.
