Support Dublin Vikings’ Storage Request – Sign the Petition!


Every June, we take part in the Dragon Boat Race with the support of the Dublin Vikings Dragon Boat. However, due to the lack of a storage container at Grand Canal Dock, it’s becoming difficult for the Vikings to provide training for different communities including Cantonese in Ireland, as equipment must be transported each time. If the storage space is granted, the Vikings will be able to offer training to anyone interested in the sport.

We urge you to sign the petition to help secure the storage space needed for our training. This will allow us to continue participating in the race with proper training from the Dublin Vikings and ensure that everyone can enjoy the sport safely and to its fullest.

Thank you for your support!
