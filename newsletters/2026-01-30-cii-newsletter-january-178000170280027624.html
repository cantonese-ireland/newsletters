Dear Cantonese lovers,

各位親愛嘅粵語愛好者︰

January in Ireland rarely feels like the start of anything new. The cold lingers, the days are short, and spring still feels far away. Yet in the traditional Irish calendar, spring begins in February — 立春, laap6 ceon1, the first solar term of the year. Even if we don’t feel it yet, change is quietly on its way.

一月嘅愛爾蘭，往往令人感受唔到「新開始」。天氣寒冷，日照短，春天似乎仲好遠。不過按照傳統二十四節氣，二月就係立春（立春，laap6 ceon1）—— 一年真正嘅開始。就算未感受到，改變其實已經悄悄開始。


As February draws near, so too does the colourful Dublin Lunar New Year Festival — a city-wide celebration of culture, performance, food, workshops, and community spanning mid-to-late February that invites everyone to enjoy the Year of the Horse together. You can find the full programme on the festival website. 

We’re delighted that Cantonese in Ireland will be part of this year’s festivities with three special events:

  1. Cantonese in Ireland Celebration Booth – Wolfe Tone Square (22  Feb) — a family-friendly cultural booth showcasing Cantonese traditions, calligraphy, Tang suit photo-ops, games, and more. 
  2. Trilingual Storytelling & Art Workshop: The Magic Horse of Han Gan (21  Feb) — a special trilingual workshop (English, French, Cantonese) co-organised with Alliance Française, featuring storytelling and a creative art session inspired by The Magic Horse of Han Gan.
  3. The Human History Behind Chinese Ma ‘Horse’ Workshop (20 Feb) — an interactive workshop exploring the cultural and historical stories of the horse in Chinese tradition. 

隨住二月臨近，都柏林農曆新年亦即將展開——一個集合文化、表演、美食、工作坊同社區活動嘅城市慶典，同大家一齊迎接馬年嘅到來。

粵語愛爾蘭好高興今年仍然可以繼續同會方合作，為大家帶嚟 三個特別節目：

  1. 粵語愛爾蘭慶祝攤位（Wolfe Tone Square）(2月22號) —— 一個適合一家大細參加嘅文化攤位，介紹廣東文化，包括書法、唐裝打卡、互動遊戲等。

  2. 三語故事及藝術工作坊：《神駒快奔》(2月21號) —— 與法國文化協會合作舉辦，以廣東話、英語同法語進行嘅故事分享及藝術創作工作。

  3. 「馬」字背後嘅人文歷史工作坊 (2月20號) —— 一個互動式工作坊，深入探討馬喺中華文化同歷史中嘅象徵同故事。

And... there’s more Hong Kong cinema coming to Ireland!

✨🍷 NIGHT KING 夜王 will hit cinemas in Ireland on 20 February 2026, just in time for Lunar New Year! From the team behind A Guilty Conscience, this highly anticipated second feature by director Jack Ng stars Dayo Wong and Hong Kong Film Awards Best Actress winner Sammi Cheng. Set against the neon-soaked nightlife of 1990s Tsim Sha Tsui, the film follows the decline of a legendary nightclub, capturing the glamour, challenges, and resilient spirit of Hong Kong.

Keep an eye on our social media and newsletter — we’ll share Irish screening details soon, and there may even be another lucky draw! 🎬
