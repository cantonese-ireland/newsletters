1st Fundraising National Mahjong Tournament 第一屆全國麻雀錦標大賽

Exciting news! Cantonese in Ireland is hosting the 1st National Mahjong Tournament!


Mahjong is the favourite pastime of a lot of Cantonese. It is a must-play at Lunar New Year celebration. Mahjong is a beloved game across East Asian regions, enjoyed by people of all ages. However, finding fellow players and Mahjong tiles in Ireland isn’t always easy. If you’ve been itching to show off your skills, here’s your chance!

Join the competition to meet fellow Mahjong enthusiasts, showcase your skills, and compete for the title of Ireland Mahjong King with a €100 Asia Market gift voucher as the grand prize!

DLNY Online Bilingual storytelling: Legend of the White snake

農曆新年網上雙語故事 : 白蛇傳

Cantonese is a Sinitic language within the Sino-Tibetan language family. Known for its vivid and colorful expressions, Cantonese brings a unique vibrancy to communication.

The Legend of the White Snake is a well-known traditional Eastern tale that tells the story of the White Snake and Green Snake transforming into human form and their experiences in the mortal world.

This Lunar New Year, the Year of the Snake, why not gather together on the first day of Dublin’s Lunar New Year celebrations to enjoy this festive and fitting folk story online?

(story will be told in both English and Cantonese)

DLNY workshop: Tang suit, Brush & Ink - A sustainable celebration of the Year of snake 

農曆新年工作坊 : 唐裝、毛筆與墨水

A sustainable celebration of the Year of snake 

This year is the Year of the Snake, and we’ll be painting lifelike snakes together using brush and ink. Ink wash painting has existed since the Tang Empire in now China, flourishing during the Song Empire and later spreading to various regions across East Asia.

After finishing the snake paintings, we can put on traditional Tang suit and take photos to celebrate the occasion!


Date: 2nd Feb (Sun)

Time: 12pm - 5pm 

Venue: Meeting House Square, East Essex Street, Temple Bar, Dublin 2

Our Volunteers 我哋嘅義工

In this issue, we would like to introduce you our volunteer - Lisa. 

喺今期嘅通訊，我哋想同大家介紹吓我哋嘅義工 Lisa. 


Tell us something about yourself.  

Hi, my name is Wai Kwan Tsang, but most people know me as Lisa Tsang. I was born and raised here in Ireland, and I specialize in social media marketing. My professional background involves creating impactful marketing strategies and promotions, which has given me a solid foundation in understanding how to connect with different audiences.

Why do you volunteer with Cantonese in ireland ?

I decided to volunteer with Cantonese in Ireland because I see how important it is to build a strong, engaged community that celebrates our culture. Being born in Ireland, I’ve always felt proud of my Cantonese heritage, and I believe it’s essential to keep our language and traditions alive. Volunteering with CII gives me the chance to combine my passion for marketing with a cause that means so much to me—helping to bring people together, share our culture, and promote diversity in Ireland.


What do you want to achieve volunteering with Cantonese in Ireland?
Through volunteering with CII, I aim to expand our reach and make sure the incredible work we’re doing—whether it’s cultural events, workshops, or community activities—gets noticed by a larger audience. I want to use my expertise in social media marketing to amplify our message, attract more people to join us, and get them involved both online and in person. Ultimately, my goal is to help grow the Cantonese community here in Ireland and create a vibrant network where people feel connected, appreciated, and inspired to celebrate our shared heritage.

不如你介紹吓自己。

Hi，我係Wai Kwan Tsang，大部分人都叫我 Lisa。我係喺愛爾蘭出生同成長嘅，專注於社交媒體營銷。我嘅專業背景係設計有影響力嘅營銷策略同推廣活動，呢啲經驗令我知道點樣同唔同背景嘅人建立聯繫。

點解你會喺我哋<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>呢個機構做義工嘅？

我選擇跟<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>做義工，係因為我知道建立一個強大、互動嘅社區，去傳揚我哋嘅文化係好重要嘅。我係喺愛爾蘭出生嘅，對自己嘅廣東血統一直好自豪，我深信我哋一定要承存我哋嘅語言同埋傳統。做義工就係一個好機會，可以將我對營銷嘅熱情同我對文化嘅關注結合，幫助將大家凝聚一齊，分享我哋嘅文化，推動多元化喺愛爾蘭嘅發展。

你喺<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>做義工嘅目標係咩？

我嘅目標係擴大我哋粵移民及粵團體係愛爾蘭嘅影響力，確保我哋嘅工作無論係文化活動、工作坊，定係社區活動，都能夠被更多人知曉。我想用我喺社交媒體營銷嘅專業知識，幫助宣傳我哋嘅訊息，吸引更多人加入。唔單止係線上，仲有線下嘅參與都好重要。最終，我嘅目標係幫助擴展喺愛爾蘭嘅粵社群，建立一個充滿活力嘅網絡，令大家覺得大家彼此連繫、互相珍惜同支持，大家一齊去慶祝我哋共同嘅文化遺產。

你有冇啲咩想同<<粵語。愛爾蘭>>講?

Irish Graduates Can Now Vote in Seanad Elections

愛爾蘭畢業生可參與參議院選舉投票

A recent Supreme Court ruling allows Irish citizens with degrees from designated institutions to vote in Seanad elections. The 2024 Seanad Electoral (University Members) Amendment Bill creates a single "Higher Education" constituency with six seats, replacing the NUI and Trinity constituencies.

Graduates can register once, even if holding multiple degrees. Registration is open until 23 January 2025 at seanadvoter.ie.

The changes won’t apply to the January 2025 election but will take effect afterward. Full details and registration forms are available online.

根據最新嘅最高法院裁決，持有指定院校學位嘅愛爾蘭公民，而家可以喺參議院選舉入面投票。《2024年參議院選舉（大學成員）修訂法案》創立咗一個全新嘅「高等教育選區」，有6個議席，取代咗之前嘅NUI同都柏林三一學院選區。

每個畢業生只可以註冊一次，即使有多個學位都係。註冊已經開放，截止日期係2025年1月23日，請到 seanadvoter.ie 登記。

呢個改動唔會影響2025年1月嘅選舉，但會喺之後嘅選舉正式生效。

TradFest 2025: Celebrating 20 Years of Irish Music and Culture

TradFest 2025：慶祝20年愛爾蘭音樂與文化

TradFest, Ireland’s premier traditional and folk music festival, marks its 20th anniversary this year! Taking place every January in Dublin, TradFest is a vibrant celebration of Irish music and culture.

Enjoy live performances from talented artists in some of Dublin’s most iconic venues, as well as in unique spaces around the city and beyond. Don’t miss this incredible opportunity to immerse yourself in the sounds and spirit of Irish tradition.

For more details, visit the official TradFest website: https://tradfest.com/

TradFest，愛爾蘭首屈一指嘅傳統及民謠音樂節，今年迎嚟第20周年！TradFest每年1月喺都柏林舉行，係一個慶祝愛爾蘭音樂文化嘅節日。

喺都柏林市中心最具代表性嘅場地，以及城市內外嘅獨特地點，我哋都可以喺呢幾日欣賞到才華橫溢嘅音樂人嘅現場表演。千祈唔好錯過呢個絕佳機會，沉浸喺愛爾蘭傳統聲音同精神！

The Last Dance movie and discussion in Chester Beatty.

(Event organised by Chester Beatty)

Ridden by debt, Hong Kong wedding planner finds a new career opportunity in running a funeral parlour. Domenic has a flair for the new business and things start to go his way, but there’s one problem – he does not get on with Master Man, a Taoist priest who performs the traditional ‘Breaking Hell’s Gate’ ritual at the funerals and is the funeral home’s part owner.
