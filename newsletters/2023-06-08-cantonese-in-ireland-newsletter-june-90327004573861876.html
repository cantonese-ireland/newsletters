Read what our director says:


Interview participant callout!

My name is Choy-Ping Clarke-Ng 吳彩萍 (Ping). I am a mixed-race Hong Kong-Irish theatre maker and designer. I have staged my own work at Dublin Theatre Festival and the Abbey Theatre (Ireland’s national theatre). Much of my work centres on identity, belonging and cross-cultural experiences. I am currently writing a new play called WANDER WANDER WILD WILD 遊遊野野. The play is based on a real plan to build a city in Ireland for 50,000 immigrants from Hong Kong.
I am looking to interview people who have recently moved from Hong Kong to Ireland in the past 10 years. The interviews would be:

  • informal
  • through English
  • an hour or so long
  • conducted in person or online
  • paid
  • participants would be credited as Research Interview Participants when the show is staged

The questions would be on things like what was the process of moving like and how are you finding life in Ireland. These interviews will help inform the writing of the play. There also is potential in future to be part of a group research session, which would also be paid. Interviews will be taking place over this summer/autumn. Please get in touch with me at choypingclarkeng@gmail.com if you would be interested in participating. Very much so looking forward to connecting.
