Lunar New Year 2023 年農曆新年

Dear Cantonese lovers, 

各位親愛嘅粵語愛好者︰

Gung1 Hei2 Faat3 Coi4 (Wish You Get Rich) is a traditional Cantonese saying to wish others a prosperous year. 

Lunar New Year is the most important festival for Cantonese people around the world. We have organised a series of activities in Ireland to celebrate. 

恭喜發財，祝大家喺愛爾蘭安居樂業，身體健康，出入平安。 今個農曆年，我哋努力籌備咗一連串嘅慶祝活動，希望各位雖然身處愛爾蘭，但仍然感受到新年氣氛。

Free Online Cantonese Course for Beginners

免費網上粵語初階課程

Cantonese is one of the most spoken Sinitic languages with about 80 million speakers around the globe. Cantonese is witty, quirky, fun and widely-used. Now here is the good chance for you to try this language. Funded by Dublin Bus Community Spirit Award, we are happy to offer a FREE Cantonese course for beginners. 

Act now! Limited spots only. 

粵語文係最多人用嘅漢語文之一，全球總共有成八千萬人口係講緊粵語㗎。粵語生鬼有趣又抵死，又多人用。宜家有個好機會去學吓呢種語言喇！多得都柏林巴士社區精神贊助基金，我哋可以為大家提供一個免費嘅粵語初階課程喇。

Lunar New Year Arts Fundraising

農曆年藝術籌款

Support a good cause

We are grateful to our young artists (aged 8-12) who spent their time and effort to create beautiful artwork for our Lunar New Year Arts Fundraising. 

In our online shop, you can browse their beautiful artworks. If you love any of them, feel free to purchase it online or contact us for more details. 

All profits go to Cantonese in Ireland. 

All artwork: 25Euro

If you would like to work with us and display your artwork in our shop, please contact us.

我哋好感恩有幾位年紀小小嘅藝術家 (八至十二歲) 用時間同心思為我哋農曆年藝術籌款活動畫咗好幾幅靚畫。

喺我哋嘅網上店裏面，你可以瀏覽到佢哋嘅藝術作品。如果你鍾意任何一畫作，你可以喺網上自行購買，或者聯絡我哋索取更多資料。
